## Fenwray Film Society

- reference/film/film-conduct.md: Behaviour at society activities, complaints and member suspensions.
- reference/film/film-equipment.md: Projector and screen loans, booking periods, deposits and returns.
- reference/film/film-fees.md: Membership fees, screening tickets, payments and concessions.
- reference/film/film-membership.md: Membership eligibility, applications, voting rights and renewal.
- reference/film/film-refunds.md: Ticket cancellations, equipment refunds, deposits and unresolved matters.
- reference/film/film-screenings.md: Public screenings, reservations, attendance and room arrangements.
- reference/film/film-changes.md: Dated committee amendments to governing sections during 2025 and 2026. Changes document. - reference/film/film-quick-guide.md: Front-desk guidance on joining, tickets, loans and cancellations. Summary; does not govern.
- reference/film/film-fees-archive.md: Superseded fee schedule retained as a historical committee record. Archive; does not govern.
