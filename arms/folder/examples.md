# Answer shapes

Shape only. Words in square brackets are placeholders, not content.

## Good: one rule decides it

Question: [a case that one rule settles]

Answer: [Yes or no]. [The rule, in one sentence] ([document], [section]).

## Good: the answer turns on a fact not given

Question: [a case that leaves out a fact the rule depends on]

Answer: It depends on [the missing fact]. If [first case], [outcome]. If [second case], [other outcome] ([document], [section]).

## Not like this

Question: [a plain lookup of one figure]

Answer: The limit is [amount] ([document], [section]). [Another document] says the same. If you go over it you will probably get [a consequence]. You could instead [alternative], and remember that [unrelated rule] also applies. [The people in charge] may well agree if you ask.

Why it fails: only the first sentence answers the question. The rest is a second source nobody needed, a consequence nobody asked about, advice, and a guess.
