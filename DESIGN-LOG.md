# Design log

Each review of `PLAN.md` before the run, what it found, and what changed. A review is done by a
session that sees the plan and the entry's design, not the reasoning behind the plan. Its notes are
sorted into mandatory, defect and optional; each is accepted, changed or refused, with the reason.
