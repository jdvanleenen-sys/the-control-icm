# question-pool.md

## P1

**Set:** film

**Type:** plain lookup

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: On 12 May 2025, a volunteer takes a £9 cash payment for a screening ticket. The customer asks whether the treasurer is required to issue a receipt. Is a receipt compulsory, and what is the payment threshold?

**Expected answer:** No, a receipt is not compulsory for a £9 payment. The treasurer must issue a receipt for payments of £10 or more, so this payment is £1 below the threshold. A receipt may be issued voluntarily, but the rules do not require one.

**Source:** film-fees.md, sections 4.1 and 4.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must identify £10 as the receipt threshold and say that a £9 payment does not require a receipt. It is wrong if it says every cash payment requires a receipt, that £9 qualifies because it is close to £10, or that receipts are issued only above £10, which would incorrectly exclude payments of exactly £10.

## P2

**Set:** film

**Type:** plain lookup

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

**Expected answer:** The reserved seat must be held until 19:40, which is 10 minutes after the advertised 19:30 start. Doors normally open at 19:00, which is 30 minutes before the advertised start. The seat-holding deadline is not the same as the door-opening time.

**Source:** film-screenings.md, sections 3.3 and 3.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give both 19:40 for the reserved-seat deadline and 19:00 for door opening. It is wrong if it gives 19:30 as the seat deadline, 19:00 as both times, or 20:00 by adding the 30-minute door-opening interval after the start.

## P3

**Set:** film

**Type:** changed rule

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

**Expected answer:** The first person pays £24 because the treasurer received payment before 1 July 2025. The second person pays £28 because the revised adult membership fee took effect on 1 July 2025. The payment receipt date, not the application acceptance date, determines which fee applies.

**Source:** film-fees.md, section 1.1; film-changes.md, sections 1.1–1.4; film-fees.md, section 4.5.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give £24 for 30 June 2025 and £28 for 2 July 2025, explaining that the payment date controls. It is wrong if it charges both £24 using the original fee schedule, both £28 using the latest rate without considering dates, or £20 using the archived schedule.

## P4

**Set:** film

**Type:** changed rule

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: An adult member booked the projector on 20 September 2025, paid the required hire fee and security deposit, and received confirmation that day. Collection is scheduled for 3 October 2025. How much security deposit must the member ultimately provide before collecting it?

**Expected answer:** The required security deposit is £100. From 1 October 2025, the deposit increased from £80 to £100, and the scheduled collection date determines which amount applies. The earlier confirmed booking does not protect the member from the increase, so an additional £20 is required if £80 was already paid.

**Source:** film-equipment.md, sections 1.4 and 3.1; film-changes.md, sections 2.1–2.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give a total deposit of £100 and identify the additional £20 due after an £80 payment. It is wrong if it says £80 because the booking was confirmed in September, or £60 because that was the archived deposit.

## P5

**Set:** film

**Type:** exception or condition

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A 17-year-old's membership application is accepted, and the treasurer receives their membership payment on 12 February 2026. On what exact date does that membership expire? Does it end in February 2026, or does the February joining rule give them longer?

**Expected answer:** The membership expires on 28 February 2027. Although membership normally lasts until 28 February following payment, someone joining during February receives membership until 28 February of the following year. The February-specific rule prevents this membership from ending only 16 days after payment.

**Source:** film-membership.md, sections 3.1–3.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must state 28 February 2027 and explain the special treatment of February enrolments. It is wrong if it gives 28 February 2026 by applying the ordinary expiry wording without the February condition, or 12 February 2027 by assuming membership lasts exactly 12 months.

## P6

**Set:** film

**Type:** exception or condition

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

**Expected answer:** No. Serious threats can result in immediate removal without a warning. Although the ordinary disruption procedure allows a volunteer to ask someone to stop and then require them to leave if they refuse, the serious-threat exception permits immediate removal.

**Source:** film-conduct.md, sections 2.1–2.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must say that no prior warning is required for a serious threat and distinguish that exception from the ordinary stop-and-refuse procedure. It is wrong if it insists that the visitor must first receive a warning or refuse an instruction before removal is permitted.

## P7

**Set:** film

**Type:** two documents

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: An active adult member wants to hire the society's projector and screen together for two consecutive booking days beginning at noon on 12 May 2026. What is the total hire charge, excluding the refundable security deposit, and when must the equipment be returned?

**Expected answer:** The total hire charge is £46, calculated as two booking days at the combined projector-and-screen rate of £23 per day. Each booking day runs from noon until noon the following day. Collection at noon on 12 May 2026 therefore means the two-day hire period ends at noon on 14 May 2026.

**Source:** film-fees.md, section 3.3; film-equipment.md, sections 2.1 and 2.2; film-changes.md, sections 4.1–4.3 (confirms that the two-day period is within the revised limit).

**Correct requires:** The answer must give £46 and a return deadline of noon on 14 May 2026, using the £23 combined rate and noon-to-noon booking days. It is wrong if it charges £52 by adding the individual projector and screen rates (£18 + £8) for each day, £38 using the archived combined rate of £19, or treats 12–14 May as three paid days and charges £69.

## P8

**Set:** film

**Type:** two documents

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

**Expected answer:** No, the suspended member is not entitled to the member discount during the suspension. Suspended members cannot claim member prices, even when their annual membership would otherwise be active. The standard admission price on 8 November 2025 is £7 rather than the £4 active-member price.

**Source:** film-membership.md, sections 5.3–5.4; film-fees.md, sections 2.1–2.2.

**Correct requires:** The answer must deny the discount and give £7 as the applicable standard admission charge, contrasting it with the £4 member price. It is wrong if it charges £4 simply because the annual membership has not expired, £5 by confusing the child ticket band or later member rate, or says suspension permanently ends the membership.

## P9

**Set:** film

**Type:** trap

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: On 10 May 2025, an adult aged 40 pays for a new annual membership. A volunteer finds an older fee sheet showing an adult subscription of £20. What amount should the treasurer actually collect?

**Expected answer:** The correct charge is £24. The £20 adult membership fee belongs to a superseded schedule that applied only through 28 February 2025. The governing schedule effective from 1 March 2025 sets the fee at £24, and the later increase to £28 does not take effect until 1 July 2025.

**Source:** film-fees-archive.md, sections 1.1, 5.1–5.2; film-fees.md, section 1.1; film-changes.md, sections 1.2–1.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give £24 and reject the archived £20 rate. It is wrong if it charges £20 from the archived document or £28 by applying the July 2025 amendment before its effective date.

## P10

**Set:** film

**Type:** trap

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

**Expected answer:** The correct ticket price is £3. The £2 price appears in the archived schedule that was superseded on 1 March 2025. The governing schedule charges accompanied children under 12 £3, and no later change alters that concession.

**Source:** film-fees-archive.md, sections 2.3 and 5.1–5.2; film-fees.md, section 2.3; film-changes.md, sections 1–6 (no replacement of the under-12 ticket rate).

**Correct requires:** The answer must give £3, noting that the child is under 12 and accompanied by an adult. It is wrong if it gives £2 from the archived schedule, £5 for the 12–15 age group, or £7 for standard admission while overlooking the applicable child concession.

## P11

**Set:** hall

**Type:** plain lookup

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: At a trustee committee meeting on 17 September 2025, seven trustees are in office but only three attend. One attendee says that three should be enough because all three agree. What is the required quorum, and can the committee make a valid decision at this meeting?

**Expected answer:** The required quorum is four trustees. Only three are present, so the meeting does not meet the quorum requirement and cannot make a valid committee decision. Unanimous agreement among those three does not make up for the missing trustee.

**Source:** hall-trustees.md, sections 2.1, 4.2 and 4.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must specify four trustees as the quorum and say three are insufficient. It is wrong if it treats three as adequate because they agree unanimously, or says the quorum is five because the committee must have at least five trustees in office.

## P12

**Set:** hall

**Type:** plain lookup

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

**Expected answer:** The total equipment charge is £25. The portable speaker costs £10 per booking and folding display boards cost £5 per set per booking. Three sets cost £15, so the speaker and boards together cost £10 + £15 = £25.

**Source:** hall-fees.md, sections 4.2–4.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give £25 and show £10 + (3 × £5). It is wrong if it charges £15 by treating all three sets of boards as one set, £13 by using the archived speaker and board prices as one-time charges, or £20 by charging for only two sets.

## P13

**Set:** hall

**Type:** changed rule

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group with 14 local members books the Meeting Room for four paid hours on 20 February 2026. The group submits its membership count before booking. What room-hire discount percentage applies, and what is the discounted room charge before any other fees?

**Expected answer:** The group receives a 25% room-hire discount because its event takes place after the revised discount became effective on 1 February 2026. Four hours in the Meeting Room cost 4 × £12 = £48 before discount. The 25% discount is £12, leaving a room charge of £36.

**Source:** hall-fees.md, sections 2.2 and 3.1–3.4; hall-changes.md, sections 3.1–3.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give a 25% discount and a final room charge of £36. It is wrong if it uses the superseded 20% discount and charges £38.40, uses the archived 15% discount and charges £40.80, or applies 25% to a different room rate.

## P14

**Set:** hall

**Type:** changed rule

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

**Expected answer:** No. From 1 April 2026, amplified music on Friday and Saturday must stop by 21:30. Because the music is being played on 4 April 2026, the revised cutoff applies, and playing until 21:45 would exceed it by 15 minutes.

**Source:** hall-conduct.md, section 4.1; hall-changes.md, sections 5.1–5.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must say no, give 21:30 as the controlling cutoff and identify the proposed 15-minute overrun. It is wrong if it permits 21:45 based on the original 22:00 cutoff, or treats the building's 23:00 Saturday closing time as permission for amplified music until 23:00.

## P15

**Set:** hall

**Type:** exception or condition

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: On 12 October 2025, an organiser wants to use both the Main Hall and Meeting Room for one event. The expected attendance is 89 people in the Main Hall and 23 in the Meeting Room, including organisers and children. Both rooms would individually be within their stated capacities. Is the combined event permitted at that attendance, and by how many people would it exceed any applicable limit?

**Expected answer:** No. The two rooms individually allow up to 90 and 24 people, but combined events are subject to a separate maximum of 100 people in the building. The proposed total is 89 + 23 = 112 people, which exceeds the combined limit by 12.

**Source:** hall-bookings.md, sections 4.1–4.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must reject the proposed attendance, give 100 as the combined maximum and calculate an excess of 12 people. It is wrong if it permits 112 people because neither room individually exceeds capacity, or uses the sum of individual limits, 114, as the permitted combined maximum.

## P16

**Set:** hall

**Type:** exception or condition

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

**Expected answer:** No, the group does not qualify for the village group discount because the rules require at least ten local members. Being registered is not sufficient when the group has only nine. On 15 October 2025, the Main Hall costs £24 per hour, so two paid hours cost £48 without a discount.

**Source:** hall-fees.md, sections 2.1, 2.4 and 3.1–3.3; hall-changes.md, sections 2.1–2.3 (confirms the £28 rate is not yet effective).

**Correct requires:** The answer must deny the discount because nine is below the ten-member minimum and give £48 as the room charge. It is wrong if it applies the original 20% discount and charges £38.40, applies the archived eight-member qualification and 15% discount to charge £40.80 at the governing hourly rate, or uses the later £28 hourly rate and charges £56.

## P17

**Set:** hall

**Type:** two documents

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Main Hall for a private celebration on 18 October 2025 and intends to serve wine to adult guests without selling it. The hirer asks whether the ordinary £30 booking deposit is sufficient, or whether any additional permission and deposit are required. What must the hirer arrange, and how much must be provided in deposits altogether?

**Expected answer:** The hirer must obtain prior written permission to serve alcohol and provide the separate £100 damage deposit. Every booking also requires the £30 booking deposit. The combined deposits are therefore £130, although the £30 booking deposit counts towards the hire charge.

**Source:** hall-conduct.md, sections 3.1–3.3; hall-fees.md, sections 5.1–5.4.

**Correct requires:** The answer must require prior written permission, identify the £100 damage deposit and the separate £30 booking deposit, and total them at £130. It is wrong if it says the £30 booking deposit alone is sufficient, treats the damage deposit as replacing the booking deposit and gives £100, or uses the archived £75 damage deposit to total £105.

## P18

**Set:** hall

**Type:** two documents

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

**Expected answer:** The attendance is permitted because the Meeting Room capacity is 24 people and the total is 21, including the adults and children. However, children under 12 must remain under adult supervision. The organiser therefore cannot leave the children unsupervised while the adults prepare another room.

**Source:** hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4; hall-safety.md, sections 3.1–3.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must give a total attendance of 21 against the capacity of 24, permit the attendance and require continuing adult supervision. It is wrong if it rejects the event by counting children and adults against separate capacity allowances, counts only the five adults, or says that being below capacity permits the 16 children to remain unsupervised.

## P19

**Set:** hall

**Type:** trap

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A trustee was elected in March 2022, re-elected in March 2024 and wants to stand again in March 2026. Is there a maximum number of consecutive two-year terms a trustee may serve, and what exactly is that maximum?

**Expected answer:** The papers do not specify a maximum number of consecutive trustee terms. They state that each trustee serves for two years and may stand again, but they explicitly leave the maximum number of terms undefined. The papers therefore do not establish a numerical cap or prohibit this trustee from standing again on term-count grounds.

**Source:** hall-trustees.md, sections 2.4 and 6.1.

**Correct requires:** The answer must explicitly say that no maximum number of terms is specified and identify the two-year term and permission to stand again as the nearby rules. It is wrong if it invents a maximum of two terms (four years), three terms (six years), or says the trustee is automatically entitled to remain in office without another election.

## P20

**Set:** hall

**Type:** trap

**Prompt (paste exactly):**

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

**Expected answer:** The papers do not say whether trustees may attend remotely or whether remote attendance counts towards the quorum. They require four trustees for a quorum but explicitly leave remote attendance unspecified. Consequently, the papers alone cannot establish whether three trustees attending in person plus one joining by video call would constitute a valid quorum.

**Source:** hall-trustees.md, sections 4.2 and 6.3.

**Correct requires:** The answer must identify four as the quorum, state that remote attendance is not addressed and decline to decide whether the proposed meeting is quorate. It is wrong if it automatically counts the video participant and declares a quorum of four, or automatically excludes that participant and declares only three without acknowledging the missing remote-attendance rule.
