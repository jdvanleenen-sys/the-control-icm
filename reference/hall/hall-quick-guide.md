# Booking Desk Quick Guide
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Using this guide
This sheet was last refreshed on 10 July 2026.
It is a convenience summary, not a governing document.
The governing documents and hall-changes.md always prevail.
Check the event and cancellation dates before quoting a price.

## 2. Rooms and prices
The Main Hall normally costs £28 an hour.
The Meeting Room costs £12 an hour.
The kitchen costs another £8 per booking.
Bookings normally last at least two hours per room.
Registered village groups generally get 25% off room hire.
Projectors cost £15 and portable speakers cost £10.
Display boards cost £5 per set.
Equipment and kitchen charges do not receive the group discount.

## 3. Booking and payment
Bookings can be requested up to a year ahead.
Allow at least three days before the event.
A booking is not accepted until confirmed in writing.
A £30 booking deposit is normally required.
The outstanding hire bill is generally due two weeks before the event.
Alcohol events also need a £100 damage deposit.

## 4. Opening and attendance
Monday to Thursday events now finish by 21:30.
Friday and Saturday events may run until 23:00.
Sunday events finish by 21:00.
The Main Hall holds up to 90 people.
The Meeting Room holds up to 24 people.
Combined events are limited to 100 people in the building.

## 5. Music and cancellations
Friday and Saturday amplified music stops at 21:30.
Music stops by 21:00 on other days.
Early cancellation may qualify for a full or partial refund.
Mid-range cancellations now generally receive 25% rather than 50%.
The booking deposit is normally kept when a hirer cancels.

## 6. Questions to refer
Check hall-refunds.md before promising any repayment.
Refer safety issues immediately to a trustee.
Do not settle disputed damage charges at the booking desk.
