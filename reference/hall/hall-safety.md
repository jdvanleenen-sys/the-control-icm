# Hall Safety Rules
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Scope
1.1 These rules were adopted on 12 February 2025.
1.2 They apply to all hirers, trustees, visitors and contractors.
1.3 The named hirer must explain emergency exits to organisers.

## 2. Fire safety
2.1 All marked fire exits must remain unobstructed.
2.2 Fire doors must not be wedged open.
2.3 Open flames, candles and fireworks are prohibited.
2.4 Electrical appliances brought in must be safe and undamaged.
2.5 Cooking is permitted only in the designated kitchen.
2.6 No cooking equipment may be placed in hired rooms.

## 3. Occupancy and supervision
3.1 Maximum numbers are specified in hall-bookings.md section 4.
3.2 The hirer must count attendees when an event begins.
3.3 Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision.
3.4 At least one responsible adult must remain near the main entrance.
3.5 Corridors and access routes must remain clear.

## 4. Emergencies and reporting
4.1 In a fire, occupants must evacuate immediately.
4.2 Emergency services must be contacted when necessary.
4.3 Occupants must assemble at the marked garden gate.
4.4 Nobody may re-enter until emergency services permit it.
4.5 Injuries requiring first aid must be recorded in the incident book.
4.6 Serious incidents must be reported to the chair within 24 hours.

## 5. Emergency spending
5.1 The chair may authorise up to £400 for urgent safety work.
5.2 This exception overrides hall-trustees.md section 5.2.
5.3 A second trustee must confirm the urgency in writing.
5.4 The spending must be reported at the next committee meeting.

## 6. Temporary closure
6.1 The chair may close an unsafe room immediately.
6.2 A closed room must not be hired until declared safe.
6.3 Refunds caused by safety closure follow hall-refunds.md section 3.
