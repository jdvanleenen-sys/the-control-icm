# Trustee Committee Rules
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 The committee manages the hall for community use.
1.2 It may hire out rooms and equipment under hall-bookings.md.
1.3 These rules were adopted on 12 February 2025.

## 2. Committee membership
2.1 The committee has between five and nine trustees.
2.2 Trustees must be at least 18 years old.
2.3 Applicants must live in Quillbrook or a neighbouring village.
2.4 A trustee serves for two years and may stand again.
2.5 Trustees receive no payment for committee work.

## 3. Appointment and departure
3.1 New trustees are elected at the annual public meeting.
3.2 Election requires more than half of votes cast.
3.3 The committee may fill vacancies until the next annual meeting.
3.4 A trustee may resign by giving written notice.
3.5 Three consecutive missed meetings without explanation trigger a review.

## 4. Meetings and votes
4.1 The committee meets at least six times each calendar year.
4.2 Four trustees form a quorum.
4.3 Decisions require a majority of those present and voting.
4.4 The chair has a casting vote if votes are equal.
4.5 Minutes must be approved at the next meeting.

## 5. Spending authority
5.1 The treasurer may approve routine spending up to £150.
5.2 Spending above £150 requires a committee vote.
5.3 Emergency safety spending follows hall-safety.md section 5.
5.4 Two trustees must approve payments above £500.

## 6. Matters not covered
6.1 These rules do not set a maximum number of trustee terms.
6.2 They do not prescribe a method for resolving personal disputes.
6.3 They do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely.
