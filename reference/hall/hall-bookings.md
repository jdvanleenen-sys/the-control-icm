# Room Booking Rules
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. General conditions
1.1 These rules were adopted on 12 February 2025.
1.2 Rooms may be hired by adults aged 18 or over.
1.3 The named hirer is responsible for the entire booking.
1.4 The hirer must give a contact address and telephone number.

## 2. Booking requests
2.1 Requests may be submitted up to 12 months in advance.
2.2 Requests must arrive at least three calendar days before the event.
2.3 The booking officer checks availability before accepting a request.
2.4 A booking is accepted only when written acceptance is issued.
2.5 Payment deadlines are set out in hall-fees.md section 6.
2.6 Failure to pay the booking deposit cancels an accepted booking.

## 3. Opening hours
3.1 Monday to Thursday: 08:00 to 22:00.
3.2 Friday and Saturday: 08:00 to 23:00.
3.3 Sunday: 09:00 to 21:00.
3.4 All guests must leave by the relevant closing time.
3.5 Setup and clearing must take place within booked hours.

## 4. Room capacities
4.1 Main Hall: maximum 90 people.
4.2 Meeting Room: maximum 24 people.
4.3 Combined events may not exceed 100 people in the building.
4.4 Capacities include organisers, performers and children.
4.5 Fire safety requirements in hall-safety.md also apply.

## 5. Access and equipment
5.1 Keys are collected from the booking officer by arrangement.
5.2 Keys may not be copied or passed to another hirer.
5.3 Equipment must be requested when the booking is made.
5.4 Equipment charges follow hall-fees.md section 4.
5.5 Hirers must report missing or faulty equipment before use.

## 6. Changes
6.1 Changes to dates or rooms require written approval.
6.2 A date change counts as cancellation and a new booking.
6.3 Cancellation charges are governed by hall-refunds.md.
