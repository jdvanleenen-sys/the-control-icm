# Hire Charges and Payments: Archived Version
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Archive status
1.1 These rules were adopted on 8 January 2025.
1.2 They applied to bookings starting from 1 February 2025.
1.3 They were superseded by hall-fees.md on 1 April 2025.
1.4 This document is retained for historical reference only.
1.5 It must not be used to quote current prices.

## 2. Room hire
2.1 Main Hall: £20 per hour.
2.2 Meeting Room: £10 per hour.
2.3 Kitchen used with either room: £6 per booking.
2.4 The minimum paid booking is two hours per room.
2.5 Setup and clearing time is included in paid hours.
2.6 Charges are rounded upwards to the next whole hour.

## 3. Discounts
3.1 Registered village groups receive 15% off room hire.
3.2 A registered village group must have at least eight local members.
3.3 Groups must confirm their membership count when booking.
3.4 Discounts apply to room charges only.
3.5 Discounts cannot be combined.

## 4. Equipment charges
4.1 Projector: £12 per booking.
4.2 Portable speaker: £8 per booking.
4.3 Folding display boards: £4 per set per booking.
4.4 Equipment may only be hired alongside a room.

## 5. Deposits
5.1 The booking deposit is £20.
5.2 Events serving alcohol require a £75 damage deposit.
5.3 The damage deposit is separate from the booking deposit.
5.4 The booking deposit counts towards the hire charge.

## 6. Payment deadlines
6.1 The booking deposit is due within seven calendar days of acceptance.
6.2 Remaining hire charges are due seven calendar days before the event.
6.3 Bookings made fewer than seven days ahead require full payment.
6.4 This archived document creates no entitlement to historical prices.
