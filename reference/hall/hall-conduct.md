# Hall Use and Conduct Rules
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Responsibility
1.1 These rules were adopted on 12 February 2025.
1.2 The named hirer is responsible for guests and suppliers.
1.3 Hirers must remain on site throughout their events.
1.4 Hirers must follow the safety rules in hall-safety.md.

## 2. Behaviour
2.1 Threatening or abusive behaviour is not permitted.
2.2 Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the building.
2.3 Illegal activities are prohibited anywhere on hall grounds.
2.4 Animals are not permitted except assistance animals.
2.5 Decorations must not be attached with nails or screws.

## 3. Alcohol
3.1 Alcohol may be served only with prior written permission.
3.2 Alcohol may not be sold under an ordinary hall booking.
3.3 Permission to serve alcohol requires the damage deposit in hall-fees.md.
3.4 The hirer must ensure alcohol is not supplied to underage guests.
3.5 Alcohol must not be taken outside the designated hall grounds.

## 4. Music and noise
4.1 Amplified music must stop by 22:00 on Friday and Saturday.
4.2 Amplified music must stop by 21:00 on all other days.
4.3 External speakers are not permitted.
4.4 Doors and windows must remain closed during amplified music.
4.5 The booking officer may require immediate noise reduction.

## 5. Cleaning and damage
5.1 Hirers must leave rooms clean and furniture in its original layout.
5.2 All rubbish must be placed in the designated outdoor bins.
5.3 Kitchen surfaces must be wiped after use.
5.4 Damage must be reported within 24 hours after the event.
5.5 Reasonable repair costs may be deducted under hall-refunds.md.

## 6. Breaches
6.1 Serious breaches may result in immediate termination of the event.
6.2 Terminated bookings receive no refund of room hire charges.
6.3 The committee may refuse future bookings following serious breaches.
