# Hire Charges and Payments
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Scope
1.1 These charges were approved on 12 February 2025.
1.2 They apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025.
1.3 Room availability is governed by hall-bookings.md.

## 2. Room hire
2.1 Main Hall: £24 per hour.
2.2 Meeting Room: £12 per hour.
2.3 Kitchen used with either room: £8 per booking.
2.4 The minimum paid booking is two hours per room.
2.5 Setup and clearing time must be included in paid hours.
2.6 Hire is charged in whole hours, rounded upwards.

## 3. Discounts
3.1 Registered village groups receive 20% off room hire.
3.2 A registered village group must have at least ten local members.
3.3 Groups must provide their membership count before booking.
3.4 The discount does not apply to kitchen or equipment charges.
3.5 No other discounts may be combined with this discount.

## 4. Equipment charges
4.1 Projector: £15 per booking.
4.2 Portable speaker: £10 per booking.
4.3 Folding display boards: £5 per set per booking.
4.4 Equipment may only be hired alongside a room.
4.5 Equipment availability must be confirmed under hall-bookings.md.

## 5. Deposits
5.1 Every booking requires a £30 booking deposit.
5.2 Parties serving alcohol require a £100 damage deposit.
5.3 The damage deposit is separate from the booking deposit.
5.4 The booking deposit counts towards the total hire charge.
5.5 Damage deposit returns follow hall-refunds.md section 4.

## 6. Payment deadlines
6.1 The booking deposit is due within seven calendar days of acceptance.
6.2 The remaining hire charge is due 14 calendar days before the event.
6.3 Bookings made fewer than 14 days ahead require full payment at acceptance.
6.4 Refund eligibility is determined under hall-refunds.md.
