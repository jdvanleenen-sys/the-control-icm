# Cancellation and Refund Rules
Quillbrook Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Scope
1.1 These rules were adopted on 12 February 2025.
1.2 They apply to accepted bookings under hall-bookings.md.
1.3 Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing.
1.4 The date the booking officer receives the request is the cancellation date.

## 2. Hirer cancellations
2.1 Cancellation at least 28 calendar days before the event gives a full refund.
2.2 Cancellation from 14 to 27 calendar days before the event gives a 50% refund.
2.3 Cancellation fewer than 14 calendar days before the event gives no refund.
2.4 The refund percentages apply to room, kitchen and equipment hire charges.
2.5 The £30 booking deposit is non-refundable in every hirer cancellation.
2.6 Refunds under 2.1 and 2.2 exclude the booking deposit.
2.7 An unpaid balance remains payable if the cancellation charge exceeds payments.

## 3. Hall cancellations
3.1 If the committee cancels an event, all payments are refunded.
3.2 This includes bookings cancelled for safety reasons under hall-safety.md.
3.3 The committee does not pay compensation for external costs.
3.4 The booking officer must notify the hirer as soon as reasonably possible.

## 4. Damage deposits
4.1 Damage deposits are returned within ten working days after the event.
4.2 Documented repair or additional cleaning costs may be deducted.
4.3 The hirer must receive an itemised statement of deductions.
4.4 Any disputed deduction is reviewed by two trustees.
4.5 An unused damage deposit is returned in full after hirer cancellation.

## 5. Refund processing
5.1 Refunds are made by bank transfer.
5.2 Approved refunds must be issued within 15 working days.
5.3 The treasurer records every refund in the payment ledger.
5.4 Refund approvals above £500 require two trustees.
5.5 Working days mean Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
