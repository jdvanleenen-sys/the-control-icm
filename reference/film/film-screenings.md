# Screening and Room Rules
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 20 February 2025
Effective: 1 March 2025

## 1. Screening schedule
1.1 The society normally holds two public screenings each month.
1.2 The committee confirms dates at least 21 days beforehand.
1.3 Screenings take place in a room hired by the society.
1.4 The society does not own or permanently control the room.
1.5 The secretary maintains the official screening calendar.

## 2. Reservations
2.1 Reservations open 14 calendar days before each screening.
2.2 Reservations close two hours before the advertised start time.
2.3 Each person may reserve no more than four tickets per screening.
2.4 Reservations are confirmed only when full payment is received.
2.5 Ticket prices are set by film-fees.md.
2.6 Unpaid requests do not reserve seats.

## 3. Capacity and entry
3.1 The maximum audience is 42 people, excluding duty volunteers.
3.2 At least two duty volunteers must attend every public screening.
3.3 Doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start time.
3.4 Reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
3.5 Late arrivals may enter only during a suitable break.
3.6 Entry is refused once the permitted audience capacity is reached.

## 4. Cancellations and changes
4.1 The coordinator may cancel a screening for safety or room unavailability.
4.2 A changed film title must be announced before doors open.
4.3 Ticket refunds are determined by film-refunds.md.
4.4 The coordinator records the reason for any cancellation.

## 5. Room and audience safety
5.1 Exit routes must be checked before the audience enters.
5.2 The room must be cleared within 30 minutes after the screening ends.
5.3 Equipment must be placed so cables do not obstruct walking routes.
5.4 The duty volunteers apply the safety rules in film-safety.md.
5.5 Audience conduct is governed by film-conduct.md.
