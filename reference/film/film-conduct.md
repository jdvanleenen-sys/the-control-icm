# Conduct and Complaints
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 18 March 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## 1. Expected behaviour
1.1 Members and visitors must treat others respectfully.
1.2 Threats, harassment and deliberate disruption are prohibited.
1.3 Visitors must follow reasonable instructions from duty volunteers.
1.4 Recording a screening without permission is prohibited.
1.5 Smoking and vaping are not permitted in the hired room.

## 2. Immediate action
2.1 A duty volunteer may ask someone to stop disruptive behaviour.
2.2 A person who refuses may be required to leave the activity.
2.3 Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning.
2.4 The duty volunteer must record the incident within 48 hours.
2.5 Immediate removal does not itself end society membership.

## 3. Complaints
3.1 Complaints may be submitted in writing to the secretary.
3.2 The secretary acknowledges a complaint within five working days.
3.3 A person named in a complaint must be allowed to respond.
3.4 The committee normally decides a complaint within 21 calendar days.
3.5 A committee member directly involved must not vote on that complaint.

## 4. Suspension
4.1 The committee may suspend membership for up to 30 calendar days.
4.2 A suspension requires a majority vote of uninvolved committee members.
4.3 The written decision must state the reason and suspension dates.
4.4 Membership rights during suspension are governed by film-membership.md.
4.5 The member may request one review within seven calendar days.
4.6 The committee decides a review within 14 calendar days.

## 5. Records and refunds
5.1 Complaint records are kept for 24 months after closure.
5.2 Access is restricted to the secretary and uninvolved committee members.
5.3 Removal from an event does not automatically create a refund right.
5.4 Refund eligibility is determined by film-refunds.md.
