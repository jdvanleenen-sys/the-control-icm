# Volunteer Front-Desk Quick Guide
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Prepared: 8 April 2025
Last reviewed: 15 July 2025

This sheet is for quick answers, not formal decisions.
The governing documents prevail over this summary.
Check film-changes.md for later decisions before quoting a figure.

## 1. Joining the society
1.1 People aged 16 and over can apply.
1.2 Under-18s need a parent or guardian's written agreement.
1.3 Adults pay roughly £25 to £30 annually.
1.4 Younger members pay £12 annually.
1.5 Membership usually runs to the end of February.
1.6 Ask the secretary if someone has recently renewed.

## 2. Coming to a film
2.1 Public screenings are normally held twice monthly.
2.2 Standard admission is £7.
2.3 Members usually pay around £4 or £5.
2.4 Children have lower prices depending on age.
2.5 Reservations open about two weeks before the film.
2.6 Seats are limited, so advance payment is recommended.

## 3. Borrowing the equipment
3.1 Adult members may borrow the projector or screen.
3.2 Bookings need a few days' notice.
3.3 The projector costs £18 per day and the screen £8.
3.4 Taking both together costs £23 per day.
3.5 Expect a refundable deposit of around £80 to £100.
3.6 The usual loan is a few days, subject to the current limit.

## 4. Cancellations and problems
4.1 Early ticket cancellation normally qualifies for a refund.
4.2 Equipment cancellation may result in a partial charge.
4.3 The society refunds tickets when it cancels a screening.
4.4 Report faulty equipment promptly to the equipment officer.

## 5. Before giving a definite answer
5.1 Check the relevant governing document and changes register.
5.2 Do not promise an exception without committee authority.
