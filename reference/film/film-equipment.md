# Equipment Lending Rules
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 26 February 2025
Effective: 1 March 2025

## 1. Eligibility and reservations
1.1 Only active members aged 18 or over may borrow equipment.
1.2 Bookings must be requested at least three calendar days ahead.
1.3 Requests are recorded in order of receipt.
1.4 A booking is confirmed when the hire fee and deposit are received.
1.5 Equipment cannot be booked for dates reserved for society screenings.

## 2. Hire period
2.1 A booking day runs from 12:00 noon until 12:00 noon the next day.
2.2 Equipment may be hired for no more than three consecutive booking days.
2.3 An extension requires approval before the original return deadline.
2.4 Extensions are charged using the rates in film-fees.md.

## 3. Deposits and collection
3.1 The refundable security deposit is £80 per booking.
3.2 One deposit covers the projector and screen when hired together.
3.3 The borrower must present their membership number at collection.
3.4 The equipment officer records its condition before handover.
3.5 The borrower signs the collection and condition record.

## 4. Use and responsibility
4.1 Equipment may be used indoors only.
4.2 Borrowers must not lend it to another person.
4.3 The projector and screen must remain in their protective cases during transport.
4.4 Borrowers must report faults within 24 hours of discovering them.
4.5 Safety precautions are specified in film-safety.md.

## 5. Returns and charges
5.1 Returns are accepted between 10:00 and 16:00 by arrangement.
5.2 A late return costs £10 for each started 24-hour period.
5.3 The deposit is returned within seven calendar days after inspection.
5.4 Documented damage costs may be deducted from the deposit.
5.5 Any deduction must be itemised and sent to the borrower.
5.6 Hire refunds are governed by film-refunds.md.
