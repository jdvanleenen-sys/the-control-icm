# Refunds and Uncovered Matters
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 25 March 2025
Effective: 1 April 2025

## 1. Member ticket cancellations
1.1 A ticket holder may request cancellation in writing.
1.2 Requests received at least 48 hours before the screening receive a full refund.
1.3 Requests received later receive no refund.
1.4 The receipt time recorded by the secretary decides which rule applies.
1.5 Refunds are made within 10 working days of approval.

## 2. Society screening cancellations
2.1 If the society cancels a screening, every paid ticket receives a full refund.
2.2 If the advertised film changes, buyers may request a full refund before entry.
2.3 A buyer who enters after being informed of the change loses that option.
2.4 No additional travel or accommodation expenses are reimbursed.

## 3. Equipment booking cancellations
3.1 A borrower may cancel by writing to the equipment officer.
3.2 Cancellation at least seven calendar days before collection receives a full hire-fee refund.
3.3 Cancellation later than that receives 50% of the hire fee.
3.4 The cancellation receipt date determines the applicable refund.
3.5 Security deposits are returned in full when collection never occurred.
3.6 Damage and late-return deductions follow film-equipment.md.

## 4. Refund administration
4.1 Refunds are issued by bank transfer unless cash repayment is agreed.
4.2 Refund requests must identify the booking or ticket concerned.
4.3 The treasurer keeps a record of the amount, reason and payment date.
4.4 Membership fee refunds are governed by film-fees.md.

## 5. Matters not covered
5.1 These rules do not establish a hardship or emergency-membership scheme.
5.2 They do not set compensation for lost personal property.
5.3 They do not specify film selection or programming criteria.
5.4 The committee considers these matters individually if they arise.
5.5 Individual decisions do not automatically create a continuing policy.
