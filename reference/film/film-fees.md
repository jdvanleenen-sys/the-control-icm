# Fees and Payments
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 12 February 2025
Effective: 1 March 2025

## 1. Membership fees
1.1 Annual adult membership costs £24.
1.2 Annual membership for people aged 16 or 17 costs £12.
1.3 Membership fees are not reduced for joining partway through the year.
1.4 The membership period is determined by film-membership.md.
1.5 No joining or administration fee is charged.

## 2. Screening tickets
2.1 Standard admission costs £7 per screening.
2.2 Members with active membership pay £4 per screening.
2.3 Children aged under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult.
2.4 Children aged 12 to 15 pay £5.
2.5 A member cannot buy discounted tickets for non-members.
2.6 Admission is subject to the capacity rules in film-screenings.md.

## 3. Equipment charges
3.1 Projector hire costs £18 per booking day.
3.2 Screen hire costs £8 per booking day.
3.3 Hiring both together costs £23 per booking day.
3.4 Only active members may borrow equipment.
3.5 Deposits, hire duration and return rules are in film-equipment.md.
3.6 A booking day is defined in section 2 of film-equipment.md.

## 4. Payment
4.1 Payments may be made by cash or bank transfer.
4.2 Cheques and instalment payments are not accepted.
4.3 Fees must be paid in full before the relevant service is provided.
4.4 The treasurer issues a receipt for payments of £10 or more.
4.5 A payment is received when cash is handed over or a transfer clears.

## 5. Refunds and adjustments
5.1 Membership fees are non-refundable after membership begins.
5.2 Ticket and equipment refunds follow film-refunds.md.
5.3 Members cannot exchange unused tickets for membership credit.
5.4 The treasurer may correct a documented overpayment at any time.
