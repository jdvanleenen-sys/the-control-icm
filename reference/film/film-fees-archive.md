# Archived Fees and Payments
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 10 January 2025
Effective: 1 February 2025
Superseded in full: 1 March 2025

This is a historical copy and must not be used for current charges.
The replacement governing document is film-fees.md.

## 1. Membership fees
1.1 Annual adult membership costs £20.
1.2 Annual membership for people aged 16 or 17 costs £10.
1.3 Membership fees are not reduced for joining partway through the year.
1.4 Membership ends on 31 January following enrolment.
1.5 No additional joining fee is charged.

## 2. Screening tickets
2.1 Standard admission costs £6 per screening.
2.2 Active members pay £3 per screening.
2.3 Children aged under 12 pay £2 when accompanied by an adult.
2.4 Children aged 12 to 15 pay £4.
2.5 Discounted tickets are for the named member only.
2.6 Admission remains subject to the room's permitted capacity.

## 3. Equipment charges
3.1 Projector hire costs £15 per booking day.
3.2 Screen hire costs £6 per booking day.
3.3 Hiring both together costs £19 per booking day.
3.4 Only active adult members may borrow equipment.
3.5 The refundable deposit is £60 per booking.
3.6 The maximum hire period is two consecutive booking days.

## 4. Payment
4.1 Payment may be made by cash or bank transfer.
4.2 Payment must be completed before tickets or equipment are issued.
4.3 Cheques are not accepted.
4.4 Receipts are provided for payments of £10 or more.

## 5. Record status
5.1 This schedule applied only from 1 February 2025 to 28 February 2025.
5.2 Later transactions are governed by film-fees.md and applicable changes.
5.3 This archived copy is kept to explain historical receipts.
