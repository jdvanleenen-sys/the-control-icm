# Membership Rules
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Adopted: 12 February 2025
Effective: 1 March 2025

## 1. Who may join
1.1 Membership is open to anyone aged 16 or over.
1.2 Applicants aged 16 or 17 need written consent from a parent or guardian.
1.3 Members do not need to live in any particular area.
1.4 Membership belongs to one person and cannot be transferred.

## 2. Applications
2.1 Applications must be made using the society's membership form.
2.2 The secretary records the date a complete application is received.
2.3 The committee decides applications within 14 calendar days.
2.4 An application is complete when the form and required consent are received.
2.5 Payment is due only after the application is accepted.

## 3. Membership period
3.1 Membership begins on the date the treasurer receives payment.
3.2 Membership lasts until 28 February following that date.
3.3 A member who joins during February receives membership until 28 February of the following year.
3.4 Renewals paid within 30 days after expiry keep the original membership number.

## 4. Rights and responsibilities
4.1 Members aged 18 or over may vote at society meetings.
4.2 Each eligible member has one vote.
4.3 Members may book equipment subject to film-equipment.md.
4.4 Membership does not guarantee admission to a screening.
4.5 Members must follow film-conduct.md at all society activities.

## 5. Ending membership
5.1 A member may resign by writing to the secretary.
5.2 The society does not refund unused membership time.
5.3 Membership may be suspended under section 4 of film-conduct.md.
5.4 A suspended member cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices.
5.5 The secretary records the start and end dates of each suspension.
