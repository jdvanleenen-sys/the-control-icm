# Committee Changes Register
Fenwray Film Society is a fictional organisation.

Maintained by the secretary.
Earlier governing text remains on file and is not rewritten.

## 1. Decision of 16 June 2025
1.1 Replaces section 1.1 Annual adult membership in film-fees.md.
1.2 From 1 July 2025, annual adult membership costs £28 instead of £24.
1.3 The date membership payment is received determines the fee.
1.4 Payments received before 1 July 2025 retain the old fee.

## 2. Decision of 10 September 2025
2.1 Replaces section 3.1 Refundable security deposit in film-equipment.md.
2.2 From 1 October 2025, the deposit is £100 instead of £80.
2.3 The scheduled collection date determines the deposit required.
2.4 Earlier confirmed bookings are subject to the new deposit if collected on or after that date.

## 3. Decision of 19 January 2026
3.1 Replaces section 2.2 Member admission in film-fees.md.
3.2 From 1 February 2026, active members pay £5 instead of £4.
3.3 The screening date determines the ticket price, including advance purchases.

## 4. Decision of 12 March 2026
4.1 Replaces section 2.2 Maximum equipment hire in film-equipment.md.
4.2 From 1 April 2026, the maximum hire is five consecutive booking days.
4.3 The scheduled first collection date determines the permitted duration.

## 5. Decision of 22 May 2026
5.1 Replaces section 3.1 Maximum audience in film-screenings.md.
5.2 From 1 June 2026, the maximum audience is 36, excluding duty volunteers.
5.3 The screening date determines the audience limit.

## 6. Decision of 14 August 2026
6.1 Replaces section 1.2 Early ticket cancellation in film-refunds.md.
6.2 From 1 September 2026, a full refund requires at least 72 hours' notice.
6.3 The scheduled screening date determines which notice period applies.
6.4 Section 1.4 of film-refunds.md still determines when notice was received.
