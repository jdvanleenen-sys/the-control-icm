**Rules that decide it**

- **Plot rules, section 3.** A household may hold up to 2 plots, with no more than 1 Standard. A household's first plot must be a Starter or Raised bed, and it may apply for a Standard plot only after holding its first plot for 1 complete plot year. Your Starter plot dates from 2023 and the Standard would be your second plot and first Standard, so you're allowed it.
