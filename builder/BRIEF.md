# The builder's brief

Written once, before the first practice round, and never edited. Its fingerprint is in
`builder/fingerprints.txt`. It is the owner's side of the `icm-architect` Build-mode dialogue,
answered in advance because no owner is present during the build.

## What you are building

An ICM workspace that answers rule questions about the papers of a small organisation, built with the
`icm-architect` skill (Build mode) in the house style of the PO Verifier. You build it and test it on
practice papers. It will later be used, unchanged except for its list of documents, on other papers of
the same kind that you will not see.

## The owner's answers to the Build-mode questions

**What is the repeating unit of work?** One question. A member, a volunteer or someone at the front
desk asks a question about a situation, under one organisation's rules. Each question is answered in a
fresh session.

**Walk me through one run.** The question arrives as the first message of the session. The papers of
two organisations sit in `reference/`, one subfolder per organisation. Each paper begins with a header
saying which organisation it belongs to and whether it is a governing document, a changes document
(later decisions that replace named sections, each with a date that decides whether it applies), a
summary, or an archive copy. The answer goes back in the reply, in one message.

**What stays the same every run, and what is new?** The papers stay the same. The question is new
every run.

**What does done look like?** A reply that answers the question from the papers.

**Who else touches this?** Nobody during a run. No person is present to answer a question from the
model.

## The limits of the harness (fixed; do not design around them)

- A session can only Read, Glob and Grep. It cannot write files, run code or reach the internet.
- Only the workspace's top-level `CLAUDE.md` loads by itself. Everything else is read when the model
  chooses to read it.
- The workspace has exactly one `CLAUDE.md`, at its top, with no `@` imports.
- Because no person is present, the workspace must not tell the model to stop and ask.
- The papers in `reference/` are never edited or moved, and nothing is added inside `reference/`.
- The list of documents is not written by hand. It is generated by `tools/make-map.mjs` from the
  papers' headers. Name in the workspace where that list lives (a stage contract or a file the map
  names, outside `reference/`), and leave a marked place for it. For practice, generate it from the
  practice papers with the same script.
- No figure, date or section number taken from any paper may appear in the workspace outside that
  generated list.

## The house style

Jeff's PO Verifier, prepared for eduba, copied to `builder/template/`: a router `CLAUDE.md`, a map
`CONTEXT.md`, numbered stage folders each with a `CONTEXT.md`, and `reference/`. Where it differs from
the skill's templates, follow the PO Verifier and list each departure in `builder/DECISIONS.md`. Copy
its structure, not its content.

## Practice

Three practice sets are in `practice/`, each with its papers and a `questions.md` holding questions,
expected answers and what a right answer requires. You may build, walk test and revise for up to five
rounds across them. Log every round in `builder/ROUNDS.md`: what you ran, what you saw, what you
changed and why. Do not read anything outside `builder/`, `practice/`, the skill, and the tools named
here.

## Before you build

Write `builder/DECISIONS.md` first: the form you chose, how many stages, where each boundary falls and
why, and each departure from the skill's templates. If the method's answer is one stage, say so and
build that.
