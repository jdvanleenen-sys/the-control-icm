# Fernhill Community Garden: Desk Volunteer FAQ

Purpose: quick answers to the things people ask most often at the Saturday desk.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. About this sheet

1.1 This is a convenience summary. If it differs from plot-rules.md, fees.md, water-and-hours.md, tool-shed.md or amendments.md, those documents govern.

1.2 If someone's case is not answered here, look it up in the governing document before answering.

## 2. Plots

2.1 Who can use the tool shed and get the gate code?
Members only. That means the plot holder and up to 2 co-gardeners named on the plot agreement, each aged 16 or over and living at the same address.

2.2 How many plots can one household have?
No more than 2, and only 1 of them can be a Standard plot. An Orchard strip counts as 2 plots, so a household with an Orchard strip cannot hold anything else.

2.3 Can a new household start with a Standard plot?
No. The first plot must be a Starter plot or a Raised bed. The household can apply for a Standard plot after holding its first plot for 1 complete plot year.

2.4 How long does someone have to fix a plot after a first notice?
14 days, counted from the date printed on the notice. They are in time if the plot meets the upkeep standard on or before the 14th day.

## 3. Money

3.1 Is the deposit charged for each plot?
No. It is $50 per household, paid once with the household's first fee.

3.2 Who gets the reduced rate?
A plot holder who was aged 65 or over on 1 January 2026, or who receives income support and shows proof dated no more than 3 months before paying. It is 50% of the annual fee, on 1 plot per household, and not on a Raised bed.

3.3 Someone wants to give up their plot in May. Do they get money back?
Yes, 50% of the annual fee they paid, provided the written notice reaches the treasurer from 1 April 2026 to 30 June 2026. From 1 July 2026 there is no refund.

## 4. Water and gate

4.1 When is the gate open?
From 06:00 to 21:00 every day from 1 March 2026 to 31 October 2026. For the rest of the year, from 08:00 to 16:30 every day.

4.2 When can people use a hose?
From 06:00 to 09:30 and from 18:30 to 21:00, for no more than 20 minutes per plot in each window (40 minutes for an Orchard strip). No hoses on a Wednesday. Watering cans are fine at any time the gate is open.

## 5. Tool shed

5.1 How often can a household take out powered equipment?
No more than 2 times in a calendar month, 1 item at a time, on Saturdays only. The person signing out must be aged 18 or over and have done the orientation during the current plot year.

## 6. Matters not covered

This sheet does not cover late-payment charges, forfeiture, water restrictions, damage charges, or the waiting list. Use the governing documents for those.
