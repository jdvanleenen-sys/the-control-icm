# Fernhill Community Garden: Water and Hours

Purpose: sets out when the garden is open, when and how water may be used, and what changes under a water restriction.

Last revised: 19 January 2026

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. Gate hours

1.1 During the growing season (plot-rules.md, section 1.4) the gate is open from 06:00 to 21:00 every day.

1.2 Outside the growing season the gate is open from 08:00 to 16:30 every day.

1.3 Everyone must be out of the garden by closing time. The gate code changes on the first Monday of each month and is given only to members (plot-rules.md, section 1.2).

## 2. Watering windows

2.1 Hoses may be used only from 06:00 to 09:30 and from 18:30 to 21:00.

2.2 No hose may be used on a Wednesday, when the tanks are refilled.

2.3 Watering cans filled from the tanks may be used at any time during gate hours, on any day.

## 3. Hose rules

3.1 A hose must be held in the hand at all times. Sprinklers, soaker hoses and unattended hoses are not allowed.

3.2 A plot may be watered by hose for no more than 20 minutes in each watering window. An Orchard strip may be watered for no more than 40 minutes in each window. Plot types are defined in plot-rules.md, section 2.

3.3 The garden has 3 standpipes, with 1 hose each. A member must hand over the hose when the time allowed in section 3.2 is up, whether or not anyone is waiting.

3.4 After use the standpipe must be turned off and the hose coiled on its stand.

## 4. Water restrictions

4.1 The committee declares a water restriction by a notice on the gate board. The restriction takes effect at 06:00 on the day after the notice is posted and lasts until the notice is taken down.

4.2 Stage 1. Hoses may be used only in the morning window in section 2.1. Odd-numbered plots may use a hose on odd dates of the month, and even-numbered plots on even dates. Section 2.2 still applies.

4.3 Stage 2. No hose may be used. Watering is by can only, with no more than 4 cans of 10 litres per plot per day. An Orchard strip is allowed no more than 8 cans per day.

4.4 A Raised bed is exempt from the odd and even date rule in section 4.2. It is not exempt from Stage 2.

4.5 A breach of a water restriction is recorded as a notice against the household and counts toward the total in plot-rules.md, section 6.1(b). It carries no period to put right.

## 5. Matters not covered

This document does not cover the upkeep standard or inspections (plot-rules.md), tool shed access (tool-shed.md), rainwater butts on individual plots, vehicle access, or lighting.
