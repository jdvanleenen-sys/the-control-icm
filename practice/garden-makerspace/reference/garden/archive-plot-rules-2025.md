# Fernhill Community Garden: Plot Rules 2025 (Archive)

SUPERSEDED by plot-rules.md on 12 January 2026. Kept for record only.

Purpose: records the plot rules as they stood for the 2025 plot year.

Last revised: 13 January 2025

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. Who can hold a plot

1.1 A plot holder is a person aged 18 or over who lives in Fernhill parish and has signed a plot agreement.

1.2 A member is a plot holder, or a co-gardener named on a plot agreement. A co-gardener must be aged 18 or over and belong to the plot holder's household. No more than 2 co-gardeners may be named on one agreement.

1.3 A household is everyone who lives at the same address.

1.4 The plot year runs from 1 January to 31 December. The growing season runs from 1 March 2025 to 31 October 2025, both dates included.

## 2. Plot types

2.1 Starter plot: 2 m by 3 m.

2.2 Standard plot: 4 m by 5 m.

2.3 Raised bed: 1.2 m by 2.4 m. Offered only to a plot holder who is aged 70 or over, or who has a mobility need accepted by the plot secretary.

2.4 Orchard strip: 3 m by 10 m. Offered only to a plot holder who has held a plot for at least 2 complete plot years.

## 3. Household limits

3.1 A household may hold no more than 3 plots in total, of which no more than 1 may be a Standard plot.

3.2 An Orchard strip counts as 2 plots for section 3.1.

3.3 A household's first plot must be a Starter plot or a Raised bed. It may apply for a Standard plot only after holding its first plot for 1 complete plot year.

## 4. Upkeep standard

4.1 At least 75% of the plot area must be planted or prepared for planting on or before 15 May 2025.

4.2 Weeds must not be taller than 20 cm.

## 5. Inspection and warnings

5.1 The plot secretary inspects every plot on the first Saturday of each month from April to October.

5.2 First notice. A plot that fails section 4 is given a written first notice. The plot holder must put the plot right on or before the 21st day after the date printed on the notice.

5.3 Second notice. If the plot is not put right in time, a second notice is issued, giving 7 days counted the same way.

5.4 Notices are counted per household, not per plot. The count returns to zero on 1 January.

## 6. Forfeiture

6.1 A plot is forfeited if:

- (a) a second notice is not put right within the 7 days in section 5.3;
- (b) the household receives 3 notices of any kind in the same plot year, even if each was put right in time;
- (c) the annual fee is still unpaid more than 30 days after its due date.

6.2 Under 6.1(b), every plot the household holds is forfeited. Otherwise only the plot concerned is forfeited.

6.3 No one in the household may apply for a plot until 6 months after the forfeiture date.

## 7. Matters not covered

This document does not cover fees, watering, tools, or the waiting list.
