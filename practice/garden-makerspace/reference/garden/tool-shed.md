# Fernhill Community Garden: Tool Shed

Purpose: sets out who may use the shed, sign-out limits, and charges for damage or loss.

Last revised: 19 January 2026

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. Who may use the shed

1.1 Only members may use the shed. Member is defined in plot-rules.md, section 1.2.

1.2 A member may not use the shed while the household has a notice that has not been put right (plot-rules.md, section 5), or an annual fee unpaid after its due date (fees.md, section 2).

1.3 The shed is open during gate hours (water-and-hours.md, section 1). The powered equipment cabinet is opened only on Saturdays from 09:00 to 13:00, by the shed volunteer.

## 2. Orientation for powered equipment

2.1 Powered equipment means the rotavator, the strimmer, the hedge trimmer and the chipper.

2.2 A member may sign out powered equipment only if aged 18 or over and holding a current orientation.

2.3 Orientation lasts 45 minutes and is held at 10:00 on the first Saturday of each month from March to September.

2.4 An orientation counts only for the plot year in which it is taken. It expires on 31 December, whatever month it was taken in.

## 3. Sign-out limits and periods

3.1 Hand tools: no more than 3 at a time per member, returned on the same day before the gate closes.

3.2 Wheelbarrows: 1 per member, for no more than 2 hours.

3.3 Powered equipment: 1 item per household at a time, for no more than 3 hours, returned on or before 13:00 on the same Saturday.

3.4 A household may sign out powered equipment no more than 2 times in a calendar month.

## 4. Sign-out log

4.1 Every sign-out is written in the sign-out log: member name, plot number, item, time out and time in.

4.2 The shed volunteer also records the log value of the item. The log value is the replacement value shown in the equipment register on the day of sign-out. A hand tool that is not in the register has a log value of $15.

4.3 The log value is fixed at sign-out. It is not changed if the register is updated later, or if the real cost of replacement turns out higher or lower.

## 5. Damage and loss charges

5.1 For a damaged item the charge is the cost of repair, but no more than 50% of the log value.

5.2 If the member writes the damage in the sign-out log when returning the item, the limit in section 5.1 is 25% of the log value instead.

5.3 For an item that is lost, or not returned more than 7 days after sign-out, the charge is 100% of the log value. Returning the item later does not cancel the charge.

5.4 Fair wear is not charged. The shed volunteer decides what is fair wear.

5.5 A charge is billed to the household and is due on or before the 14th day after the date of the bill. An unpaid charge may be taken from the deposit as set out in fees.md, section 3.2.

## 6. Matters not covered

This document does not cover personal tools kept on a plot, fuel, first aid, or how to become a shed volunteer.
