# Fernhill Community Garden: Amendments Log

Purpose: records each rule change agreed by the committee since the working documents were last revised.

Last revised: 22 June 2026

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. How to read this log

1.1 From its effective date, an amendment overrides the section it names.

1.2 The other documents have not been rewritten to match. They still show the wording as it stood before each amendment.

1.3 Each entry names the date that decides whether the amendment applies to a particular case. If that date falls before the effective date, the section as originally written applies to that case.

1.4 An amendment changes only the section it names. Every other section stays in force as written.

## 2. Amendment 1

2.1 Effective date: 1 April 2026.

2.2 Changes: fees.md, section 4 (Late-payment charge). The whole of section 4 is replaced.

2.3 New rule. A fee not paid on or before its due date carries a late-payment charge of $2 for each day it remains unpaid, counting the day after the due date as day 1. The late-payment charge is capped at $30 per plot in a plot year. The late-payment charge is not reduced by fees.md, section 6, and is not refundable. Paying it does not stop forfeiture under plot-rules.md, section 6.1(c).

2.4 Trigger: the due date of the fee. The amendment applies to a fee whose due date falls on or after 1 April 2026. A fee that fell due before 1 April 2026 stays under the original section 4, even if it is still unpaid on or after that date.

## 3. Amendment 2

3.1 Effective date: 1 June 2026.

3.2 Changes: water-and-hours.md, section 2 (Watering windows). The whole of section 2 is replaced.

3.3 New rule. Hoses may be used only from 06:00 to 09:00 and from 19:00 to 21:00. No hose may be used on a Monday, when the tanks are refilled. Hoses may be used on a Wednesday. Watering cans filled from the tanks may be used at any time during gate hours, on any day.

3.4 Trigger: the date the watering takes place. The amendment applies to watering on or after 1 June 2026. Where another section refers to the morning window or to the day on which no hose may be used, it is read with the new times and the new day from that date.

## 4. Amendment 3

4.1 Effective date: 1 July 2026.

4.2 Changes: plot-rules.md, section 5.2 (First notice).

4.3 New rule. A plot that fails plot-rules.md, section 4, is given a written first notice. The plot holder must put the plot right on or before the 10th day after the date printed on the notice.

4.4 Trigger: the date printed on the first notice. The amendment applies to a first notice dated on or after 1 July 2026. A first notice dated on or before 30 June 2026 keeps the period in the original section 5.2, even if that period ends in July.

## 5. Matters not covered

This log does not cover changes agreed but not yet in force, or the committee's reasons for each change.
