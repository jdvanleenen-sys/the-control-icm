# Fernhill Community Garden: Fees

Purpose: what a household pays for its plots, when it is due, and what is returned.

Last revised: 12 January 2026

Fernhill Community Garden is fictional.

## 1. Annual fees

1.1 The annual fee is charged per plot for each plot year. Plot types are defined in plot-rules.md, section 2.

- Starter plot: $30
- Standard plot: $85
- Raised bed: $20
- Orchard strip: $140

## 2. Due dates

2.1 The annual fee for the 2026 plot year is due on or before 15 February 2026.

2.2 For a plot taken up during the year, the fee is due on or before the 14th day after the date of the plot agreement. The full annual fee is charged if the agreement is dated on or before 30 June 2026. Half the annual fee is charged if it is dated 1 July 2026 or later.

## 3. Deposit

3.1 The deposit is $50 per household, not per plot. It is paid once, with the household's first fee.

3.2 The deposit is returned within 30 days after the household gives up its last plot, less any unpaid fees and charges, including charges under tool-shed.md, section 5.

3.3 The deposit is kept in full if any plot of the household is forfeited under plot-rules.md, section 6.1.

## 4. Late-payment charge

4.1 A fee not paid on or before its due date carries a late-payment charge of $5 for each complete week (7 days) it remains unpaid after the due date. There is no charge for a part week.

4.2 The late-payment charge is capped at $20 per plot in a plot year.

4.3 The late-payment charge is not reduced by section 6 and is not refundable. Paying it does not stop forfeiture under plot-rules.md, section 6.1(c).

## 5. Refunds

5.1 A plot holder who gives up a plot in writing is refunded as follows:

- notice received on or before 31 March 2026: the annual fee paid, less a $10 administration charge;
- notice received from 1 April 2026 to 30 June 2026, both dates included: 50% of the annual fee paid;
- notice received on or after 1 July 2026: no refund.

5.2 The date that counts is the date the written notice reaches the treasurer, not the date the plot is cleared.

5.3 No annual fee is refunded on a forfeited plot.

## 6. Reduced rate

6.1 The reduced rate is 50% of the annual fee in section 1.1.

6.2 A plot holder qualifies who was aged 65 or over on 1 January 2026, or who receives income support and shows proof dated no more than 3 months before the date of payment.

6.3 The reduced rate applies to 1 plot per household. If the household holds 2 plots, it applies to the plot with the lower annual fee. A Raised bed is always charged the full fee in section 1.1 and is left out when choosing the plot under this section.

6.4 The reduced rate and the half fee in section 2.2 can both apply to the same plot. The plot holder then pays 25% of the annual fee.

## 7. Matters not covered

This document does not cover forfeiture (plot-rules.md), how tool damage charges are worked out (tool-shed.md), donations, or payment methods.
