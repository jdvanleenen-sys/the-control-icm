# Front Desk Quick Guide

Purpose: gives front-desk volunteers short answers to the questions members ask most often.

Last revised: 9 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Using this guide

### 1.1 Status

This guide is a convenience summary. If it differs from the other documents, the other documents govern. Each answer names the section it summarises.

## 2. Questions and answers

### 2.1 How old must someone be to join?

Aged 16 or over on the day of joining. A member aged 16 or 17 needs a signed consent form and may hold the Bench tier only. (membership.md section 2.1)

### 2.2 How many guests may a member bring?

Up to 2 at a time, each aged 12 or over. One person may be a guest on no more than 3 days in a calendar month. (membership.md section 3.1)

### 2.3 May a guest use a machine?

A guest aged 16 or over may, during the host's own booked session, if the host holds a valid induction for that machine and stays at it. (membership.md section 3.2)

### 2.4 How far ahead may a member book?

Bench up to 21 days, Forge up to 14 days, Keystone up to 7 days. The day of booking is day zero. (machine-booking.md section 2.2)

### 2.5 How late may a member check in?

Within 15 minutes after the session start time. Exactly 15 minutes is in time. After that it is a no-show. (machine-booking.md section 5.1)

### 2.6 When may a booking be cancelled without charge?

24 hours or more before its start time. (machine-booking.md section 4.1)

### 2.7 Which machines need an induction?

The laser cutter, CNC router, table saw, wood lathe, MIG welder and kiln. The vinyl cutter and 3D printers do not. (safety-inductions.md section 1.1)

### 2.8 What does paying late cost?

£2.00 for each overdue day, up to £24.00 an invoice. (fees.md section 4.2)

### 2.9 How much is the deposit and when is it returned?

£60.00, returned within 28 days after the termination date, less anything owed. (fees.md section 3.1)

### 2.10 How long may work stay on the project shelf?

Up to 14 days. The day placed is day one. (storage.md section 1.2)

### 2.11 How long is an overdue item kept in the holding cage?

21 days. The day it is moved is day one. (storage.md section 3.2)

### 2.12 Someone has been hurt. What happens first?

Tell the duty staff member at once and write the injury in the incident book before the injured person leaves the building. (incidents.md section 1.1)

## 3. Not covered

This guide does not cover damage charges, suspension and termination, tier changes, refunds, or anything a volunteer should pass to the Workshop Manager.
