# Storage

Purpose: the lockers and shelves members may use, what may be kept there, for how long, and what happens to items left too long.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Lockers and shelf space

### 1.1 Entitlement

There are 60 lockers and 24 shelf bays. A Forge member has one locker. A Keystone member has one locker and one shelf bay. A Bench member has neither. When a change of tier (membership.md section 1.1) removes an entitlement, 4.2 applies as if the last day on the old tier were the termination date.

### 1.2 Project shelf

Any member in good standing (membership.md section 2.3) may leave work in progress on the project shelf, labelled with their name and the date placed, for up to 14 days. The day placed is day one and the item must be gone by the close of day 14.

## 2. What may be stored

### 2.1 Limits

A locker may hold no more than 25 kg. Nobody may store food, pressurised gas cylinders, lithium batteries rated above 100 Wh, or more than 500 ml in total of flammable liquid. Exactly 25 kg, exactly 100 Wh and exactly 500 ml are allowed.

### 2.2 Prohibited items

Staff remove a prohibited item when they find it. Section 3 then applies, the day of removal being the day the item is moved to the holding cage.

## 3. Items left past the limit

### 3.1 Holding cage

An item still on the project shelf after the limit in 1.2 is moved to the holding cage, a locked area beside the front desk, on the following day. The owner is emailed that day. A holding charge is made under fees.md section 2.4.

### 3.2 Holding period

The item is kept in the holding cage for 21 days. The day it is moved is day one and day 21 is included. The owner may collect it on any of those days.

### 3.3 Uncollected items

On the day after the holding period ends, the item becomes the property of the makerspace. It is offered at the members' sale held on the first Saturday of the following calendar month. The sale price, less the holding charge, is paid to the former owner. An unsold item is scrapped.

## 4. Suspension and termination

### 4.1 Suspension

A suspended member's locker and shelf bay contents may stay where they are. The member may make one accompanied visit, by appointment, to collect items. Items on the project shelf remain subject to 1.2.

### 4.2 After termination

A locker and shelf bay must be emptied within 10 days after the termination date (membership.md section 5.3). The day after the termination date is day one and day 10 is included. Anything left is moved to the holding cage on day 11, and section 3 applies.

## 5. Not covered

This document does not cover parking, the tool library, or insurance of members' belongings.
