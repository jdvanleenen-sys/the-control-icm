# Machine Booking

Purpose: which machines are booked, how far ahead, for how long, and what follows a cancellation or no-show.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Bookable machines

### 1.1 List

The bookable machines are the laser cutter, CNC router, table saw, wood lathe, MIG welder, kiln, vinyl cutter and the four 3D printers (P1 to P4). All other equipment is used without booking. Which machines need an induction is in safety-inductions.md section 1.1.

## 2. Booking windows

### 2.1 Who may book

Only a member in good standing (membership.md section 2.3) may book. A machine needing an induction also requires a valid induction on the day of the session, not the day of booking (safety-inductions.md section 3), or a supervisor under safety-inductions.md section 4.

### 2.2 How far ahead

Bookings are made on the booking board, at the front desk or online. A Bench member may book up to 21 days ahead, a Forge member up to 14 days and a Keystone member up to 7 days. The day the booking is made is day zero, and the last day of the window is included.

### 2.3 Weekly limit

In any week, Monday to Sunday inclusive, a member may hold booked sessions totalling no more than 4 hours (Bench), 10 hours (Forge) or 20 hours (Keystone). 3D printer sessions do not count.

## 3. Sessions

### 3.1 Length

The workshop is open from 08:00 to 22:00 every day. Sessions start on the hour or the half hour and last at least 30 minutes. The longest single session is 2 hours on the laser cutter, 12 hours on a 3D printer or the kiln, and 4 hours on any other bookable machine.

### 3.2 Peak time

Peak time is 17:00 to 22:00 Monday to Friday and all day Saturday. A session with any part in peak time is a peak session. Every other session is an off-peak session.

## 4. Cancellation

### 4.1 Notice

A booking cancelled 24 hours or more before its start time is cancelled without charge, and its hours stop counting towards 2.3. A booking cancelled later is a late cancellation: its hours still count towards 2.3 and it is charged under fees.md section 2.2.

## 5. No-shows

### 5.1 Check-in

A member must check in at the front desk within 15 minutes after the session start time; checking in at exactly 15 minutes is in time. A member who does not is a no-show, and the session is released to other members.

### 5.2 Consequences

A no-show is charged under fees.md section 2.2 and its hours still count towards 2.3. After a second no-show in one calendar month the member may book no more than 2 days ahead for 30 days. The day after the second no-show is day one and day 30 is included.

## 6. Not covered

This document does not cover block bookings for classes, maintenance closures, or equipment used without booking.
