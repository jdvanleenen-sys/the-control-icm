# Fees

Purpose: what members pay, when payment is due, the charge for paying late, and refunds.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Monthly fee

### 1.1 Amounts

The monthly fee is £28.00 for Bench, £54.00 for Forge and £95.00 for Keystone.

### 1.2 Included hours

Each calendar month a Forge member has 6 included machine hours and a Keystone member has 15. A Bench member has none. Included hours are applied to sessions in order of start time. Unused hours are not carried forward.

## 2. Machine-hour and other charges

### 2.1 Hourly rates

Booked time beyond the included hours is charged in half-hour units at £4.00 an hour for an off-peak session and £6.50 an hour for a peak session (machine-booking.md section 3.2). The laser cutter and CNC router carry a further £2.00 an hour on every booked hour, included hours too. The vinyl cutter and 3D printers are free and use no included hours.

### 2.2 Late cancellations and no-shows

A late cancellation (machine-booking.md section 4.1) costs half of what 2.1 would charge for the booked time with no included hours. A no-show (machine-booking.md section 5.1) costs the whole of that amount plus £5.00. Neither uses included hours.

### 2.3 Inductions

Each induction costs £15.00 a person.

### 2.4 Holding charge

The holding charge is £1.50 an item for each day the item is in the holding cage during the holding period (storage.md sections 3.1 and 3.2).

## 3. Deposit

### 3.1 Amount and return

Every member pays a deposit of £60.00 on joining. It is returned within 28 days after the termination date (membership.md section 5.3), less anything the member owes. The day after the termination date is day one and day 28 is included.

## 4. Late payment

### 4.1 Due dates

The monthly fee is due on the 1st of the month. All other charges are invoiced on the last day of the month and are due on the 1st of the next month. The due date is day one. An invoice not paid in full by the close of day 8 is overdue, and day 9 is the first overdue day.

### 4.2 Late-payment charge

A late-payment charge of £2.00 is made for each overdue day on which any part of the invoice is unpaid at the close of the day, up to a cap of £24.00 an invoice, reached on the twelfth overdue day. The charge is not itself subject to a late-payment charge. What follows when the cap is reached is in membership.md section 4.1.

## 5. Refunds

### 5.1 Rules

No part of a monthly fee is refunded on resignation, termination or change of tier. If a session is lost because the machine is out of service, its charge is refunded and any included hours used are restored.

## 6. Not covered

This document does not cover class tickets, material prices, or gift vouchers.
