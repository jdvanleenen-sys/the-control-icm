# Safety Inductions

Purpose: which machines need an induction, who may give one, how long it lasts, supervised use and the machine log.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Machines needing an induction

### 1.1 List

An induction is needed for the laser cutter, CNC router, table saw, wood lathe, MIG welder and kiln. No induction is needed for the vinyl cutter or the 3D printers.

### 1.2 Age limit

Nobody under 18, member or guest, may be inducted on or operate the table saw or the MIG welder.

## 2. Who may give an induction

### 2.1 Inductors

An induction may be given by the Workshop Manager, by a technician, or by a member inductor. A member inductor is a member aged 18 or over who has at least 40 meter hours of logged sessions on that machine and has been approved for it in writing by the Workshop Manager. No more than 3 people may be inducted together. The fee is in fees.md section 2.3.

## 3. Validity

### 3.1 Validity period

An induction is valid from the day it is given. It lapses at the end of the day falling 12 months after the holder's most recent logged session on that machine, or 12 months after the induction if no session has been logged since. Months are counted date to date: a last session on 10 March 2026 keeps the induction valid up to and including 10 March 2027.

### 3.2 Suspension and lapse

Days on which the holder is suspended (membership.md section 4) are not counted: the lapse date moves later by one day for each day of suspension. A lapsed induction cannot be extended; the member takes a new one.

## 4. Supervised use

### 4.1 Supervisor

A member without a valid induction may use a machine listed in 1.1 only with a supervisor at the machine for the whole session. The supervisor must be someone who may give inductions on that machine under 2.1, and may supervise one member at a time. Guests are covered by membership.md section 3.2 instead.

### 4.2 Limit

A member may have no more than 4 supervised sessions on any one machine. A supervised session is booked in the learner's name.

## 5. Machine log

### 5.1 Entries

Each machine listed in 1.1 has a log book and an hour meter. At the start and at the end of every session the user writes their name, the time and the meter reading. A logged session is a session with both entries. A technician records each service in the same log, with the meter reading.

### 5.2 Service intervals

The service interval, in meter hours, is 150 for the laser cutter, 200 for the CNC router, 100 for the table saw, 250 for the wood lathe, 120 for the MIG welder and 300 for the kiln.

## 6. Not covered

This document does not cover first aid, fire drills, or protective equipment.
