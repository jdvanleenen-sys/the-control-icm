# Members' Handbook 2025 (extract)

Purpose: preserves an extract of the combined handbook that members worked from during 2025.

Last revised: 6 January 2025

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Status of this extract

### 1.1 Superseded

This handbook is superseded by the current documents. It is kept for record only and must not be used to answer a question about the rules now in force.

## 2. Membership

### 2.1 Tiers

There are three tiers: Bench, Forge and Keystone.

### 2.2 Joining

Anyone aged 18 or over on the day of joining may join.

### 2.3 Guests

A member in good standing may host up to 2 guests at a time. A guest must be aged 12 or over and must sign in at the front desk.

## 3. Booking

### 3.1 Sessions

The workshop is open from 08:00 to 22:00 every day. Sessions start on the hour or the half hour and last at least 30 minutes.

### 3.2 Peak time

Peak time is 17:00 to 22:00 Monday to Friday and all day Saturday. A session with any part in peak time is a peak session.

### 3.3 Cancellation

A booking cancelled 12 hours or more before its start time is cancelled without charge.

## 4. Inductions

### 4.1 Machines

An induction is needed for the laser cutter, CNC router, table saw, wood lathe, MIG welder and kiln. No induction is needed for the vinyl cutter or the 3D printers.

### 4.2 Group size

No more than 3 people may be inducted together.

## 5. Storage

### 5.1 Project shelf

A member in good standing may leave work in progress on the project shelf, labelled with their name and the date placed, for up to 7 days. The day placed is day one and the item must be gone by the close of day 7.

### 5.2 Prohibited items

Nobody may store food or pressurised gas cylinders.

## 6. Fees

### 6.1 Monthly fee

The monthly fee is due on the 1st of the month. No part of a monthly fee is refunded on resignation.

### 6.2 Deposit

Every member pays a deposit of £40.00 on joining.

## 7. Incidents

### 7.1 Injuries

Every injury, however small, is reported at once to the duty staff member named on the front-desk board and written in the incident book before the injured person leaves the building.

### 7.2 Damage

A person who causes or finds damage to a machine stops work, tags the machine "Out of use" and tells the front desk the same day.

## 8. Not covered

This extract does not reproduce the 2025 fee table, the 2025 booking limits, the rules on suspension and termination, or the rules on damage charges.
