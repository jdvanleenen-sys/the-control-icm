# Incidents

Purpose: how injuries and damage are reported, who pays for damage, and how the damage charge is worked out.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Injuries

### 1.1 Reporting

Every injury, however small, is reported at once to the duty staff member named on the front-desk board. It is written in the incident book before the injured person leaves the building. A machine involved is switched off and tagged "Out of use" until a technician releases it.

## 2. Damage

### 2.1 Reporting

A person who causes or finds damage to a machine stops work, tags the machine "Out of use", writes the damage in the machine log (safety-inductions.md section 5.1) and tells the front desk the same day.

### 2.2 Damage found at the start

Damage written in the machine log before the user's own start-of-session meter reading is not attributed to that user.

## 3. Who pays

### 3.1 Ordinary use and misuse

The makerspace pays for damage arising in ordinary use and for damage that cannot be attributed. Damage arising from misuse is charged under section 4. Misuse means use against what the induction taught, use of a material not permitted on that machine, use with a guard removed, or use by a person with neither a valid induction nor a supervisor.

### 3.2 The person charged

The charge falls on the member in whose name the session was booked. If a guest caused the damage, it falls on the host. In a supervised session (safety-inductions.md section 4) it is split equally between the learner and the supervisor, unless the supervisor is the Workshop Manager or a technician, in which case the learner pays half and the makerspace bears the rest.

## 4. Damage charge

### 4.1 Repair cost

The repair cost is the price of parts as invoiced to the makerspace, plus technician labour at £30.00 an hour in half-hour units, rounded up.

### 4.2 Hours since service

Hours since service are the meter reading written at the start of the session in which the damage happened, minus the meter reading at the most recent service entry before it. Both readings come from the machine log.

### 4.3 Share charged

The share depends on hours since service as a proportion of that machine's service interval (safety-inductions.md section 5.2):

- up to and including half the interval: the whole repair cost;
- more than half, up to and including the whole interval: 60% of the repair cost;
- more than the whole interval: nothing.

### 4.4 Cap and payment

The charge for one incident is capped at £350.00 before any split under 3.2. It is invoiced with the other charges under fees.md section 4.1.

## 5. Not covered

This document does not cover damage to members' own projects, theft, or insurance claims.
