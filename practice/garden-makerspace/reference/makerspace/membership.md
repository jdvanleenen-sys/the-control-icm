# Membership

Purpose: who may be a member, the tiers, guests, suspension and termination.

Last revised: 2 March 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. Tiers

### 1.1 Tier names

There are three tiers: Bench, Forge and Keystone. Booking rights are in machine-booking.md section 2, storage in storage.md section 1.1 and fees in fees.md section 1. A member may change tier from the first day of any calendar month.

## 2. Who may join

### 2.1 Age

Anyone aged 16 or over on the day of joining may join. A member aged 16 or 17 needs a consent form signed by a parent or guardian and may hold the Bench tier only.

### 2.2 Join date

The join date is the day on which both the first monthly fee and the deposit (fees.md section 3.1) have been paid.

### 2.3 Good standing

A member is in good standing when not suspended and with no invoice overdue under fees.md section 4.1.

## 3. Guests

### 3.1 Numbers and age

A member in good standing may host up to 2 guests at a time. A guest must be aged 12 or over and must sign in at the front desk. One person may be a guest on no more than 3 days in a calendar month.

### 3.2 Guests and machines

A guest aged 16 or over may operate a bookable machine during the host's own booked session if the host holds a valid induction for it (safety-inductions.md section 3) and stays at the machine throughout. The age limit in safety-inductions.md section 1.2 applies. A guest under 16 may use hand tools only. Damage by a guest is covered by incidents.md section 3.2.

## 4. Suspension

### 4.1 Grounds

A member is suspended:

(a) automatically, from the day after the late-payment charge on any invoice reaches its cap (fees.md section 4.2); or

(b) by the Workshop Manager for a safety breach, for a stated period of 7 to 60 days, both inclusive.

### 4.2 Effect and end

A suspended member may not enter the workshop, book a machine or host a guest. Monthly fees continue to fall due. Stored items are covered by storage.md section 4.1. A suspension under 4.1(a) ends on the day after every overdue invoice and late-payment charge has been paid.

## 5. Termination

### 5.1 Resignation

A member may resign in writing. Notice given on or before the 20th of a month ends the membership on the last day of that month; later notice ends it on the last day of the following month.

### 5.2 Termination by the makerspace

The makerspace ends a membership at the close of day 60 of a suspension under 4.1(a), the first day of suspension being day one.

### 5.3 Termination date

The termination date is the last day of membership under 5.1 or 5.2.

## 6. Not covered

This document does not cover corporate memberships, volunteer duties, or how the makerspace is governed.
