# Bulletin

Purpose: records each rule change made since the other documents were last revised.

Last revised: 24 August 2026

Brindle Yard Makerspace is fictional.

## 1. How this bulletin works

### 1.1 Effect

From its effective date, an entry overrides the section it names. The other documents have not been rewritten to match.

### 1.2 Which rule applies

Each entry names a deciding date. If the deciding date for a case falls on or after the entry's effective date, the entry applies. If it falls earlier, the section as printed applies.

## 2. Entries

### 2.1 Entry 1

Effective date: 1 May 2026.

Changes: machine-booking.md section 5.1.

New rule: A member must check in at the front desk within 10 minutes after the session start time; checking in at exactly 10 minutes is in time. A member who does not is a no-show, and the session is released to other members.

Deciding date: the date of the session, whenever the booking was made.

### 2.2 Entry 2

Effective date: 1 June 2026.

Changes: fees.md section 4.2.

New rule: A late-payment charge of £3.00 is made for each overdue day on which any part of the invoice is unpaid at the close of the day, up to a cap of £36.00 an invoice, reached on the twelfth overdue day. The charge is not itself subject to a late-payment charge. What follows when the cap is reached is in membership.md section 4.1.

Deciding date: the due date of the invoice (fees.md section 4.1). An invoice due earlier keeps the printed rate and cap for all of its overdue days.

### 2.3 Entry 3

Effective date: 15 July 2026.

Changes: storage.md section 3.2.

New rule: The item is kept in the holding cage for 14 days. The day it is moved is day one and day 14 is included. The owner may collect it on any of those days.

Deciding date: the date the item is moved to the holding cage.

### 2.4 Entry 4

Effective date: 1 September 2026.

Changes: safety-inductions.md section 3.1.

New rule: An induction is valid from the day it is given. It lapses at the end of the day falling 9 months after the holder's most recent logged session on that machine, or 9 months after the induction if no session has been logged since. Months are counted date to date: a last session on 10 September 2026 keeps the induction valid up to and including 10 June 2027.

Deciding date: the date of the holder's most recent logged session on that machine, or of the induction if there has been none since. An induction with an earlier deciding date keeps the printed period until the holder next logs a session on that machine.

## 3. Not covered

This bulletin does not cover temporary closures, staff changes, or proposals not yet in force.
