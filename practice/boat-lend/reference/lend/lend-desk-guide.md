# Front-Desk Quick Guide
This organisation is fictional.
Prepared on 2 June 2025; retained without updating on 8 August 2026.

## What this sheet is for
This is a reminder, not the rulebook, and some figures below are now out of date.
The governing documents prevail over this guide.
Apply any relevant replacement in lend-changes.md before giving a final answer.

## Joining
Membership is for local adults, with room for 2 adults from the same household.
Ask for a recent document showing the applicant's name and address.
The usual annual fee is £24, or £12 for a household declaring income support.
Membership runs for a year once approval and payment are complete.
Check lend-membership.md and lend-fees.md before accepting payment.

## Borrowing
A household can have 4 items, but only 2 of them can be tools.
Ordinary loans are for a fortnight, with one extra week if nobody is waiting.
Powered tools need a £20 deposit each and a first-time handover for the collecting adult.
Write down the actual due date, allowing for a closed desk.
Check lend-borrowing.md and lend-safety.md when booking the loan.

## Returns and problems
Late items cost £1 a day each, up to £10 each.
An unused first membership can be refunded if the request arrives within a week.
Tool deposits normally come back within a week of return and inspection.
Do not promise a refund or charge for damage without checking lend-refunds.md.
Record unsafe behaviour and use lend-conduct.md for complaints or suspensions.
