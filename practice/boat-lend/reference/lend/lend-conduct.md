# Conduct and Complaints
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 20 May 2025; effective from 1 June 2025.

## 1 Using the annexe
### 1.1 Respect for others
Do not threaten, insult or harass members, visitors or volunteers.
Keep the doorway and marked walking route clear of bags and borrowed items.
### 1.2 Children and animals
Children under 12 must remain with an accompanying adult.
Animals must stay outside, except assistance animals accompanying their handler.

## 2 Desk decisions
### 2.1 Immediate pause
A desk volunteer may stop a collection or ask someone to leave if behaviour is unsafe.
The volunteer must record the reason and tell the committee within 2 days.
### 2.2 Suspension
The committee may suspend borrowing for up to 30 calendar days after reviewing the incident.
It must give the household a written reason and start and end dates.
A suspension does not cancel existing return dates or charges.

## 3 Complaints and review
### 3.1 Making a complaint
Send a written complaint to the library email address or leave it in a sealed envelope at the desk.
The committee must acknowledge it within 7 days and reply within 21 days of receipt.
### 3.2 Appealing a decision
Ask for review within 14 days of receiving a suspension or charge decision.
Two committee members not involved in that decision must reply within 21 days of the request.
### 3.3 Financial effect
Refunds follow lend-refunds.md; a conduct suspension does not itself create a refund entitlement.
An unpaid-charge block remains in place during an appeal, as stated in lend-fees.md.
