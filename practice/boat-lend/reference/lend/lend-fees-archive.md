# Archived Fees and Deposits
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 6 January 2025; effective from 13 January 2025.
Superseded by lend-fees.md on 1 March 2025; not authority for current charges.

## 1 Opening-period payments
### 1.1 Payment method
Pay in pounds by cash before collecting a loan.
The desk gives a handwritten receipt showing the date and amount.
### 1.2 Ordinary loans
There is no separate charge for an ordinary toy or tool loan.

## 2 Annual membership
### 2.1 Standard annual fee
The annual household membership fee is £18 for each 12-month term.
The fee is payable on joining and on each renewal.
### 2.2 Reduced annual fee
The annual fee is £9 if either named adult declares that the household receives income support.
Record the declaration on the application; no benefit letter is required.

## 3 Tool deposits
### 3.1 Deposit amount
Collect a refundable £15 deposit for each powered tool before handing it over.
No deposit is required for toys or hand tools.
### 3.2 Repayment
Repay the deposit within 10 calendar days after return and inspection.
Give written details of any deduction for missing parts or damage.
### 3.3 Record status
Keep this file to explain opening-period receipts, not to calculate later payments.
For current decisions, read lend-fees.md with the applicable entries in lend-changes.md.
