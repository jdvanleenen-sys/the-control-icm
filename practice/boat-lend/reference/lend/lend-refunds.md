# Refunds and Replacement Charges
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 12 February 2025; effective from 1 March 2025.

## 1 Deposits
### 1.1 Deposit repayment
Repay a powered-tool deposit within 7 calendar days after return and inspection.
Repay it by the original payment method unless the member agrees otherwise.
### 1.2 Deductions
Deduct only a confirmed repair or replacement charge for that tool from its deposit.
Give the member a written calculation; any balance owed remains payable.

## 2 Membership refunds
### 2.1 Unused membership
Refund the full annual fee if a written request reaches the desk within 7 calendar days of joining.
This applies only if neither named adult has collected any item during that membership.
The joining date is day zero when counting the request period.
### 2.2 Other cancellations
There is no membership refund after an item has been collected or for an unused renewal.
A conduct suspension does not qualify for a refund.
### 2.3 Refund payment
Pay an approved membership refund within 14 calendar days of receiving the request.

## 3 Loss and damage
### 3.1 Assessment
Do not charge for ordinary wear; charge actual repair cost for avoidable damage, up to £40 per item.
For a lost or irreparable item, charge its recorded replacement value, capped at £60 per item.
### 3.2 Notice and payment
Give written notice of the calculation; payment is due within 14 days of that notice.
Overdue charges remain separate; appeals follow lend-conduct.md and waivers follow lend-fees.md.
