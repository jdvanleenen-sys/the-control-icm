# Fees and Deposits
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 12 February 2025; effective from 1 March 2025.

## 1 Paying at the desk
### 1.1 Payment methods
Pay in pounds by cash or card before collecting a loan.
The desk gives a receipt showing the payment date and what the payment covers.
### 1.2 No ordinary loan fee
There is no separate charge for an ordinary toy or tool loan.
Late, missing and damaged items are dealt with under lend-borrowing.md.

## 2 Annual membership
### 2.1 Standard annual fee
The annual household membership fee is £24 for each 12-month term.
The fee is payable on joining and on each renewal.
### 2.2 Reduced annual fee
The annual fee is £12 if either named adult declares that the household receives income support.
No benefit letter is required; record the declaration on the application.
The reduced fee cannot be combined with any other fee reduction.

## 3 Deposits and records
### 3.1 Tool deposit
Collect a refundable £20 deposit for each powered tool before handing it over.
No deposit is required for toys or hand tools.
Deposit deductions and repayment times are set out in lend-refunds.md.
### 3.2 Unpaid charges
An unpaid charge blocks new loans and renewal until paid or formally waived.
A pending appeal does not remove this block.
### 3.3 Approval of waivers
Only the committee may waive a charge, with the amount and reason recorded in its minutes.
