# Borrowing Rules
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 12 February 2025; effective from 1 March 2025.

## 1 Taking items home
### 1.1 Household limit
A household may hold up to 4 items, including no more than 2 tools.
Each item with its own stock number counts as one item, even if it contains several pieces.
### 1.2 Eligibility
Membership must be active, and all charges must be paid or formally waived.
Powered tools also require the deposit in lend-fees.md and the handover in lend-safety.md.

## 2 Loan periods
### 2.1 Ordinary loan
An ordinary loan lasts 14 calendar days from its collection date.
The desk writes the due date on the receipt.
### 2.2 One extension
The desk may grant one extension of 7 calendar days if nobody has reserved the item.
Ask before the current due date; an overdue loan cannot be extended.
### 2.3 Closed return dates
If a due date falls when the desk is closed, return by the next advertised opening.
An extension runs from the recorded due date, including any closure adjustment.

## 3 Reservations and late returns
### 3.1 Reservations
Each household may reserve up to 2 items; collection is due within 7 days of notification.
### 3.2 Overdue charge
Charge £1 per item for each calendar day after its recorded due date, capped at £10 per item.
### 3.3 Missing items
After 28 days overdue, the committee may declare an item lost and apply lend-refunds.md.
