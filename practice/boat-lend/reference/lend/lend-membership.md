# Membership Rules
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 12 February 2025; effective from 1 March 2025.

## 1 Who may join
### 1.1 Age and address
Members must be at least 18 and live in the fictional town of Osterby.
Bring one document showing your name and current address, dated within the last 3 months.
### 1.2 Household membership
One membership covers up to 2 named adults living at the same address.
Children may use borrowed toys but cannot hold membership or collect loans.

## 2 Starting and renewing
### 2.1 Membership term
Membership lasts 12 calendar months from the joining or renewal date.
Renewal starts on the date the renewal payment is accepted, not on the old expiry date.
### 2.2 Activation
Membership starts only when the application is approved and the annual fee is paid.
Annual fees and concessions are set out in lend-fees.md.
A member with an unpaid charge cannot renew until it is paid or formally waived.

## 3 Records and permissions
### 3.1 Named borrowers
Either named adult may collect items and is responsible for following lend-borrowing.md.
The first adult named on the application is responsible for household charges.
### 3.2 Changes and expiry
Tell the desk about an address or contact change within 14 days.
Expired memberships cannot take new loans; existing return dates still apply.
### 3.3 Reading these rules
Read these rules with the other governing files and applicable entries in lend-changes.md.
An applicable replacement in lend-changes.md prevails over the original section.
