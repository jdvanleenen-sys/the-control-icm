# Safety and Item Care
This organisation is fictional.
Adopted on 20 May 2025; effective from 1 June 2025.

## 1 Before collection
### 1.1 Desk check
A volunteer must check the item against its parts list before every loan.
Items with cracks, exposed wires or missing safety guards must not be lent.
### 1.2 Powered-tool handover
Each collecting adult must receive a 10-minute handover before their first powered-tool loan.
Record the handover date on that adult's membership record.
A handover does not replace the manufacturer's instructions or teach a trade.

## 2 Using borrowed items
### 2.1 Instructions and age labels
Follow the supplied instructions and the age label on each toy.
Only the named adults on the membership may operate borrowed powered tools.
### 2.2 Faults and incidents
Stop using an item immediately if it appears faulty or causes an injury.
Notify the desk within 24 hours of discovering the fault or incident.
Do not repair, alter or lend the item to another household.

## 3 Returns and limits
### 3.1 Clean and complete
Return items dry, reasonably clean and with every listed part.
Report missing parts at return; charges are assessed under lend-refunds.md.
### 3.2 Matters not covered
These papers do not set rules for delivery, commercial use or loans to schools.
They do not set a procedure for accepting donated items.
No permission or promised service on those matters can be inferred from these papers.
