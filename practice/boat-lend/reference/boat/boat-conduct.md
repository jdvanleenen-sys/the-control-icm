# Member Conduct Rules

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Expected behaviour

### 1.1 Respect
Members and guests must treat other lake users, volunteers and neighbours respectfully.

### 1.2 Instructions
Members must follow reasonable instructions from an on-duty committee member.

### 1.3 Alcohol and drugs
No person may launch or operate a club boat while affected by alcohol, illegal drugs, or medication that makes safe operation unlikely.

## 2. Club property

### 2.1 Care
Members must use boats, paddles, buoyancy aids and keys with reasonable care.

### 2.2 Damage
Any damage, loss or suspected defect must be reported before leaving the site.

### 2.3 Unauthorised removal
Club equipment must not be removed from the lake site without written committee approval.

## 3. Harassment and abuse

### 3.1 Prohibited conduct
Threatening, discriminatory, abusive or harassing behaviour is not allowed.

### 3.2 Immediate response
An on-duty committee member may require a person to stop boating and leave the site for that day.

## 4. Complaints

### 4.1 Making a complaint
A complaint should be sent to the Club Secretary within 14 days of the event.

### 4.2 Review
The committee will aim to acknowledge a complaint within seven days.

## 5. Suspension

### 5.1 Temporary suspension
The committee may suspend a member’s access for up to 30 days while reviewing serious conduct concerns.

### 5.2 Longer action
Any suspension longer than 30 days requires a recorded committee decision.

### 5.3 Safety link
A safety breach may also lead to action under boat-safety.md.
