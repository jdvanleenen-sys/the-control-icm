# Membership Rules

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Membership classes

### 1.1 Individual membership
Individual membership is available to a person aged 18 or over.

### 1.2 Household membership
A household membership may include up to two adults and four children who normally live at the same address.

### 1.3 Junior membership
A junior membership is available to a person aged 12 to 17. A parent or guardian must approve the application.

## 2. Joining

### 2.1 Application
Applications must be submitted using the club form and include an emergency contact.

### 2.2 Approval
Two committee members must approve an application before a membership account is activated.

### 2.3 Start date
Membership begins on the date the Treasurer records cleared payment of the joining fee and annual fee.

## 3. Member records

### 3.1 Contact details
Members must keep their email address, telephone number and emergency contact current.

### 3.2 Review
The Membership Secretary may ask a member to confirm their details once each calendar year.

## 4. Guests

### 4.1 Guest limit
An adult member may bring up to two guests on any one visit.

### 4.2 Responsibility
The member remains responsible for each guest’s conduct and compliance with boat-safety.md.

### 4.3 Repeated use
A guest may use club boats on no more than three separate days between 1 January and 31 December.

## 5. Ending membership

### 5.1 Resignation
A member may resign by email to the Membership Secretary.

### 5.2 Suspension
The committee may suspend access under section 5 of boat-conduct.md.

### 5.3 Unpaid accounts
A membership account is inactive while fees remain unpaid under boat-fees.md.
