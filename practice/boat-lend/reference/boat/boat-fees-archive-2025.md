# Fees and Payments Rules: Archive Version

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Archive status

### 1.1 Superseded document
This version applied from 1 January 2025 until 31 December 2025.

### 1.2 Current rules
For current fees, members must use boat-fees.md and boat-changes.md.

### 1.3 Record purpose
This file is retained to explain historic payments and refunds.

## 2. Fees during 2025

### 2.1 Joining fee
A new member paid a one-time joining fee of £10.

### 2.2 Annual individual fee
The annual fee for an individual membership was £45.

### 2.3 Annual household fee
The annual fee for a household membership was £70.

### 2.4 Annual junior fee
The annual fee for a junior membership was £20.

## 3. Boat-use charges during 2025

### 3.1 Standard session
A booked rowing boat or kayak session cost £3 per boat.

### 3.2 Junior session
A junior session with an approved adult supervisor cost £1 per boat.

### 3.3 Payment timing
Boat-use charges were due before launch unless the Treasurer agreed otherwise.

## 4. Refunds during 2025

### 4.1 Club cancellation
The club refunded boat-use charges in full when it cancelled a confirmed booking.

### 4.2 Member cancellation
A member could receive a refund when cancelling at least 48 hours before the session start time.

### 4.3 No-show
No refund was due for a missed booking or late cancellation.
