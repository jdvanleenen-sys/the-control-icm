# Water Safety Rules

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Before launch

### 1.1 Buoyancy aids
Every person in a club boat must wear a properly fitted club-approved buoyancy aid.

### 1.2 Equipment check
The booking holder must check the boat, paddle or oars, and buoyancy aids before launch.

### 1.3 Defects
A boat with a suspected defect must not be launched and must be marked unavailable.

## 2. Young people

### 2.1 Under-12s
A child under 12 must share a boat with an adult member aged 18 or over.

### 2.2 Ages 12 to 17
A junior member aged 12 to 17 may use a boat only while an approved adult member remains on site.

### 2.3 Guest children
Guest children count toward the limits in section 4 of boat-membership.md.

## 3. Lake limits

### 3.1 Operating area
Club boats must remain within the marked boating area.

### 3.2 Distance
Boats must remain at least 10 metres from the reed beds and nesting platforms.

### 3.3 Night use
No club boat may be used from 30 minutes before sunset until sunrise.

## 4. Conditions

### 4.1 Closure authority
An on-duty committee member may close the lake to club boating because of wind, lightning, poor visibility, ice, water quality concerns or rescue activity.

### 4.2 Member decision
Members must not launch if they reasonably believe conditions are unsafe, even if the lake is open.

### 4.3 Cancellation
A closure cancels affected bookings for the purpose of section 4.1 of boat-fees.md.

## 5. Incidents

### 5.1 Immediate action
In an emergency, call the emergency services first where needed.

### 5.2 Reporting
All incidents, near misses and equipment failures must be reported to the Safety Officer within 24 hours.
