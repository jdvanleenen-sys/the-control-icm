# Volunteer and Committee Rules

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Volunteer roles

### 1.1 Committee members
The committee includes a Chair, Treasurer, Club Secretary, Membership Secretary, Safety Officer and Equipment Officer.

### 1.2 Duty volunteers
A duty volunteer may open the site, check bookings, give routine safety reminders and report defects.

### 1.3 Limits of authority
A duty volunteer may not change fees, approve membership, or impose a suspension longer than one day.

## 2. Training

### 2.1 Induction
A volunteer must complete a site induction before taking an opening or closing duty.

### 2.2 Record
The Safety Officer must keep a record of inductions completed from 1 January 2025 onward.

### 2.3 Refresher
Opening-duty volunteers must complete a refresher briefing at least once every 24 months.

## 3. Opening and closing

### 3.1 Opening check
Before opening, the duty volunteer must check the weather notice, rescue equipment and visible boat condition.

### 3.2 Closing check
After closing, the duty volunteer must check that boats are secured and the equipment store is locked.

### 3.3 Unsafe conditions
The duty volunteer must contact an on-duty committee member if conditions suggest closure under boat-safety.md.

## 4. Records

### 4.1 Booking records
The Treasurer may use booking records to reconcile boat-use charges under boat-fees.md.

### 4.2 Incident records
The Safety Officer must retain incident reports for two years from the report date.

## 5. Vacancies

### 5.1 Temporary cover
The committee may appoint a temporary volunteer to cover a vacant role until the next committee meeting.

### 5.2 Not covered
This document does not set election procedure, meeting frequency, or rules for spending donated funds.
