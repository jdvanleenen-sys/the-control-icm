# Front-Desk Quick Guide

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Use this guide

### 1.1 Purpose
This guide helps volunteers answer common questions quickly.

### 1.2 Legal status
The governing documents and boat-changes.md prevail if this guide differs from them.

### 1.3 Check changes
Always check boat-changes.md before quoting a fee, refund rule or booking limit.

## 2. Membership basics

### 2.1 Who can join
Adults can join individually, households can join together, and young people aged 12 to 17 can join as juniors.

### 2.2 Younger children
Children under 12 must be in a boat with an adult member.

### 2.3 Guests
An adult member can bring up to two guests on one visit, but the member is responsible for them.

## 3. Booking basics

### 3.1 Timing
Members can normally book from 14 days before a session until two hours before it starts.

### 3.2 Sessions
A normal booking gives two hours, including getting the boat out and putting it away.

### 3.3 Arrival
Tell members to arrive 10 minutes early; after 15 minutes, an unused boat may be reassigned.

## 4. Safety basics

### 4.1 Buoyancy aids
Everyone in a boat wears a buoyancy aid, without exceptions.

### 4.2 Weather
Volunteers may close the lake if conditions are unsafe.

### 4.3 Problems
Damage, near misses and incidents should be reported immediately, then formally within 24 hours.

## 5. Fees and behaviour

### 5.1 Payment
Members should pay before launching unless the Treasurer has agreed another arrangement.

### 5.2 Cancellations
Refund timing depends on boat-fees.md and any applicable entry in boat-changes.md.

### 5.3 Conduct
Ask people to be respectful, follow volunteer instructions and never boat while impaired.
