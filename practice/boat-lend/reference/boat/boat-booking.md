# Boat Booking Rules

Brindlecombe Community Boat Club is a fictional organisation.

## 1. Who may book

### 1.1 Active members
Only an active member under boat-membership.md may make a booking.

### 1.2 Junior bookings
A junior member may book only where an approved adult member is named in the booking.

### 1.3 Guests
Guests cannot make bookings and must be included under the responsible member’s booking.

## 2. Booking window

### 2.1 Opening time
Bookings open at 8:00 am, 14 days before the session date.

### 2.2 Closing time
Bookings close two hours before the session start time.

### 2.3 Session length
A standard session lasts two hours, including launch and return time.

## 3. Limits

### 3.1 Weekly limit
A member may hold no more than three future bookings in any rolling seven-day period.

### 3.2 Boat limit
A member may book up to two boats in one session.

### 3.3 Priority
Committee-led training may reserve boats up to 21 days in advance.

## 4. Arrival and return

### 4.1 Arrival
The booking holder must arrive at least 10 minutes before launch.

### 4.2 Unclaimed boats
A boat may be released to another member 15 minutes after the booking start time.

### 4.3 Return
All boats must be returned, cleaned and secured before the session end time.

## 5. Cancellations

### 5.1 Member cancellation
Members must cancel promptly through the booking system or by contacting a committee member.

### 5.2 Fee refunds
Refund entitlement is determined by section 4 of boat-fees.md.

### 5.3 Unsafe conditions
A booking may be cancelled under section 4 of boat-safety.md.
