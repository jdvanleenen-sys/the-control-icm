# Booking and Deposits

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 3 March 2025. In force from 1 April 2025.

## 1 Making a booking
1.1 Bookings are taken up to 12 months ahead and no later than 7 days before the hire date. Where alcohol will be sold the limit is 14 days (hall-licensing-limits.md, section 2.2).
1.2 A provisional booking is held for 10 days. It lapses if it has not been confirmed by then.
1.3 A booking is confirmed when the signed booking form and the booking fee have been received and the booking secretary has issued a written confirmation.
1.4 The booking date is the date on that confirmation. The hire date is the day of the event.

## 2 Paying
2.1 The booking fee is 25% of the hire charge, with a minimum of £20. It counts towards the hire charge.
2.2 The balance of the hire charge and the damage deposit are due 28 days before the hire date.
2.3 Where the booking date is fewer than 28 days before the hire date, everything is paid in full at booking.
2.4 If the balance is more than 7 days late, the hall may treat the booking as cancelled by the hirer, with notice received on the eighth day. hall-cancellations-and-damage.md, section 1, then applies.

## 3 Damage deposit
3.1 The damage deposit is £100 for the Main Hall and £50 for the Garden Room or the Committee Room. It is £250 for any hire where alcohol is sold or supplied, where more than 80 guests are expected, or which is a party under section 2 of hall-who-can-hire.md.
3.2 One deposit is taken for each hire: the highest that applies.
3.3 The deposit is returned within 14 days after the hire date, less any deductions under hall-cancellations-and-damage.md, section 4.
3.4 A regular hirer pays one standing deposit of £100 for each agreement year in place of a deposit for each hire. The higher deposit in section 3.1 is still taken for any hire to which it applies.

## 4 Keys
4.1 Keys are collected from the booking secretary no more than 24 hours before the hire starts. They are returned by 10:00 on the day after the hire date.
4.2 A lost key is charged at £45.

## 5 Hire times
5.1 Rooms can be hired between 08:00 and midnight. Guests must leave earlier than that (hall-licensing-limits.md, section 4).
5.2 The hire period must include the time needed for setting up and clearing away.
5.3 Staying past the booked end time is an overrun and is charged under hall-hire-charges.md, section 4.1.
