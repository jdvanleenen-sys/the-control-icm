# Licensing Limits

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 3 March 2025. In force from 1 April 2025.

## 1 Capacity
1.1 The most people allowed are: Main Hall 120 standing or 90 seated at tables; Garden Room 30; Committee Room 12.
1.2 No more than 150 people may be in the building at once, whatever rooms are hired.
1.3 The count includes children, helpers, caterers and performers.

## 2 Alcohol
2.1 The hall's premises licence allows the sale of alcohol from 12:00 to 23:00 Monday to Saturday and from 12:00 to 22:30 on Sunday.
2.2 Alcohol may be sold only with the committee's written consent, asked for at least 14 days before the hire date.
2.3 Alcohol that the hirer supplies free, or that guests bring for themselves, needs no consent but must be declared on the booking form.
2.4 No alcohol is allowed at a party under section 2 of hall-who-can-hire.md.
2.5 A hirer who wants to sell alcohol outside the hours in 2.1 must obtain a temporary event notice at the hirer's own cost. The committee allows no more than 6 such events in a calendar year.

## 3 Music
3.1 Music, live or recorded, ends at 23:00 Monday to Saturday and at 22:00 on Sunday.
3.2 A sound limiter is set at 92 decibels. It cuts the power to the stage sockets and must not be bypassed.
3.3 Doors and windows are kept shut after 21:00 while amplified music is playing.

## 4 End of the event
4.1 Guests leave the building by 23:30.
4.2 The hirer finishes clearing up, locks the building and leaves by midnight.

## 5 Safety
5.1 Fire exits and the corridors leading to them are kept clear at all times.
5.2 The hirer tells guests where the fire exits and the assembly point are at the start of the event.
5.3 An event with more than 80 guests needs one steward aged 18 or over for every 40 guests or part of 40.

## 6 Breaches
6.1 A committee member may end an event that breaks any limit in this document. No hire charge is refunded.
6.2 Deductions from the damage deposit for a breach are set out in hall-cancellations-and-damage.md, section 4.3.
