# Hire Charges

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 9 June 2025. Applies to hires with a hire date on or after 1 September 2025. It replaces the charges kept in hall-hire-charges-archive.md.

## 1 Hourly rates
1.1 The rate for each hour depends on the hirer's category (hall-who-can-hire.md, section 1.2):

| Room | Resident or community group | Non-resident | Commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Hall | £18 | £26 | £38 |
| Garden Room | £10 | £15 | £22 |
| Committee Room | £7 | £10 | £15 |

1.2 The shortest hire is 2 hours. Part hours are charged as full hours.
1.3 Full use of the kitchen costs a flat £15 for each booking. Light use is free (hall-kitchen-and-equipment.md, section 1).
1.4 A booking with a booking date before 9 June 2025 keeps the charge quoted on its confirmation, whatever its hire date.

## 2 Session rates
2.1 Saturday evening in the Main Hall, 18:00 to midnight, kitchen included: £110 resident or community group, £160 non-resident, £230 commercial.
2.2 Whole day in the Main Hall, 08:00 to midnight, kitchen included: £220 resident or community group, £310 non-resident, £450 commercial.
2.3 A session rate is charged in place of the hourly rates wherever it comes to less.

## 3 Discounts
3.1 Regular hirers get 10% off hourly rates. The discount does not apply to session rates or extras.
3.2 A funeral tea booked by a resident has no hire charge for up to 3 hours in any one room. The kitchen charge and extras are still paid.
3.3 Only one discount can be used for a hire.

## 4 Extras
4.1 Overrun: £25 for each started 30 minutes in the Main Hall, and £15 in the other rooms.
4.2 Stage and sound system: £20 for each booking. Projector and screen: £10 for each booking.
4.3 On a bank holiday all hourly and session rates are increased by 50%.
4.4 Heating is included in every rate.

## 5 Review
5.1 The committee reviews charges once a year. The next review is on 8 June 2026, and any new charges start on 1 September 2026.
