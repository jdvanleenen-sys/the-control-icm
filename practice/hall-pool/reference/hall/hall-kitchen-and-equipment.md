# Kitchen and Equipment

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 3 March 2025. In force from 1 April 2025.

## 1 Using the kitchen
1.1 Light use means the kettle, the hot water urn, the fridge and the sink. It comes free with any room hire.
1.2 Full use means the cooker, ovens, dishwasher, crockery and cutlery. The kitchen must be booked and the kitchen charge paid (hall-hire-charges.md, section 1.3).
1.3 A hirer preparing food for sale to the public must hold a Level 2 food hygiene certificate dated within the last 3 years, and must show it to the booking secretary at least 7 days before the hire date.
1.4 Deep-fat fryers and hirers' own gas appliances are not allowed.

## 2 Furniture and equipment
2.1 Every room hire includes use of the hall's 20 folding tables and 120 chairs. None of it may leave the building.
2.2 Electrical equipment brought in by a hirer must carry a safety test label dated within the last 12 months.
2.3 Bouncy castles are allowed in the Main Hall only, up to 3.5 metres high. The supplier must hold public liability insurance of at least £5 million and the hirer must show proof at least 7 days before the hire date.
2.4 Smoke machines, confetti and candles are not allowed. Candles on a cake are allowed.
2.5 The stage, sound system and projector are extras and must be booked (hall-hire-charges.md, section 4.2).

## 3 Clearing up
3.1 Crockery and cutlery are washed, dried and put away. Tables and chairs are stacked where they were found.
3.2 Floors are swept. The hall takes 2 sacks of rubbish; the hirer takes away the rest.
3.3 If a room or the kitchen is left needing cleaning, the cleaning charge in hall-cancellations-and-damage.md, section 4.2, is taken from the damage deposit.

## 4 Breakages
4.1 Broken crockery and glasses are charged at £2 an item and written on the sheet by the kitchen door.
4.2 Any other damage to equipment is charged at the cost of repair or replacement.

## 5 Storage
5.1 A regular hirer may rent a storage cupboard for £5 a month.
5.2 Anything left behind by any other hirer is kept for 14 days after the hire date and then disposed of.
