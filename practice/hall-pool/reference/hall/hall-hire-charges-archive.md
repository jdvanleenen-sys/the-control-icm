# Hire Charges (Superseded Version)

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Superseded. Kept for the record only. Adopted by the Management Committee on 6 January 2025 and applied to hires with a hire date from 6 January 2025 to 31 August 2025. Replaced by hall-hire-charges.md for hires on or after 1 September 2025.

## 1 Hourly rates
1.1 The rate for each hour depends on the hirer's category:

| Room | Resident or community group | Non-resident | Commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Hall | £16 | £23 | £35 |
| Garden Room | £9 | £13 | £20 |
| Committee Room | £6 | £9 | £14 |

1.2 The shortest hire is 1 hour. Part hours are charged as full hours.
1.3 Full use of the kitchen costs a flat £12 for each booking. Light use is free.

## 2 Session rates
2.1 Saturday evening in the Main Hall, 18:00 to midnight, kitchen included: £100 resident or community group, £145 non-resident, £210 commercial.
2.2 Whole day in the Main Hall, 08:00 to midnight, kitchen included: £200 resident or community group, £280 non-resident, £410 commercial.
2.3 A session rate is charged in place of the hourly rates wherever it comes to less.

## 3 Discounts
3.1 Regular hirers get 15% off hourly rates. The discount does not apply to session rates or extras.
3.2 A funeral tea booked by a resident is charged at half the hourly rate for up to 3 hours.
3.3 Only one discount can be used for a hire.

## 4 Extras
4.1 Overrun: £20 for each started 30 minutes in the Main Hall, and £10 in the other rooms.
4.2 Stage and sound system: £15 for each booking. The projector and screen are free.
4.3 On a bank holiday all hourly and session rates are increased by 25%.
4.4 Heating is included in every rate.
