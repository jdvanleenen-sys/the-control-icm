# Who Can Hire the Hall

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 3 March 2025. In force from 1 April 2025.

## 1 Hirers
1.1 The hirer is the person who signs the booking form. The hirer must be aged 21 or over.
1.2 Every hirer is placed in one category: resident (lives in Tannerwick parish, shown by proof of address dated within the last 3 months); community group (not for profit, with at least half its members living in the parish); non-resident; or commercial (any hire run for profit, whoever the hirer is).
1.3 The category decides the hire charge. See hall-hire-charges.md, section 1.
1.4 The hirer, or a named deputy aged 21 or over, must be in the building for the whole hire.

## 2 Parties for young people
2.1 Parties for a 16th, 17th or 18th birthday are taken only from resident hirers.
2.2 There must be one adult aged 25 or over present for every 15 guests or part of 15.
2.3 No alcohol may be brought, supplied or sold at these parties. The higher damage deposit in hall-booking-and-deposits.md, section 3.1, applies however many guests are expected.

## 3 Regular hirers
3.1 A regular hirer is one hirer with 10 or more confirmed bookings in any 12 months.
3.2 Regular hirers sign a yearly agreement. The first agreement year runs from 1 September 2025 to 31 August 2026.
3.3 A regular hirer gets the discount in hall-hire-charges.md, section 3.1, and the standing deposit in hall-booking-and-deposits.md, section 3.4. A hirer who drops below 10 bookings keeps regular status until the agreement year ends.

## 4 Refusing a hire
4.1 The booking secretary may refuse any hire and must give the reason in writing within 7 days.
4.2 A hirer who has owed the hall money for more than 30 days cannot book until it is paid.
4.3 A refused hirer may appeal in writing to the chair within 14 days of the refusal. The committee decides at its next meeting.

## 5 Matters not covered
These papers say nothing about the matters below. Ask the booking secretary, who will take the question to the committee.
- Insurance for a hirer's own belongings and for a hirer's guests.
- Use of the car park, including overnight parking.
- Hire of the playing field beside the hall.
- Wedding ceremonies, as distinct from wedding receptions.
- Filming or photography for publication.
