# Changes Log

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

The governing documents are not reworded when the committee changes a rule. Read them together with this log. Where an entry applies to a case, it replaces the section it names; where it does not, the old wording stands.

## Change 1: Damage deposit amounts
- Decided on 9 June 2025. Effective from 1 July 2025.
- Replaces section 3.1 of hall-booking-and-deposits.md. Sections 3.2 to 3.4 are unchanged.
- New rule: the damage deposit is £150 for the Main Hall and £50 for the Garden Room or the Committee Room. It is £300 for any hire where alcohol is sold or supplied, where more than 80 guests are expected, or which is a party under section 2 of hall-who-can-hire.md.
- Deciding date: the booking date. A booking with a booking date on or after 1 July 2025 pays the new amounts. An earlier booking keeps the old amounts, whatever its hire date.

## Change 2: Late cancellations
- Decided on 13 October 2025. Effective from 1 November 2025.
- Replaces sections 1.4 and 1.5 of hall-cancellations-and-damage.md. Sections 1.1 to 1.3 and 1.6 are unchanged.
- New rule: notice received 28 to 15 days before the hire date, 50% of the hire charge is kept. Notice received 14 days or fewer before the hire date, or no notice at all, the whole hire charge is kept.
- Deciding date: the date the cancellation notice is received. Notice received on or after 1 November 2025 falls under the new rule, whatever the booking date or hire date.

## Change 3: Music finishing time
- Decided on 12 January 2026. Effective from 1 February 2026.
- Replaces section 3.1 of hall-licensing-limits.md. The £50 deduction for late music then runs from the new times.
- New rule: music, live or recorded, ends at 22:30 Monday to Saturday and at 22:00 on Sunday.
- Deciding date: the hire date. A hire on or after 1 February 2026 follows the new times, even if it was booked earlier.

## Change 4: Kitchen charge
- Decided on 9 March 2026. Effective from 1 April 2026.
- Replaces section 1.3 of hall-hire-charges.md. Session rates still include the kitchen.
- New rule: full use of the kitchen costs a flat £20 for each booking. Light use stays free.
- Deciding date: the hire date. A hire on or after 1 April 2026 pays £20, except that a booking with a booking date before 9 March 2026 keeps the £15 charge.
