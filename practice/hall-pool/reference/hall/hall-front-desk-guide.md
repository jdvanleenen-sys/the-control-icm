# Front Desk Quick Guide

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Last updated on 17 November 2025. This sheet is a plain-words reminder for whoever is answering the phone. It is not the rules. If it differs from the governing documents, read together with hall-changes-log.md, the governing documents prevail.

## Who can book
- Anyone 21 or over. Locals and village groups pay the lowest rate; businesses pay the most.
- Teenage birthday parties are for village families only, with plenty of adults present and no drink.
- Groups who book ten or more times a year count as regulars and get money off.

## Booking and paying
- We can pencil a date in for about a week and a half. It is not theirs until they have paid the booking fee and had our confirmation.
- The booking fee is a quarter of the hire charge. The rest, plus the deposit, is due about a month before.
- We need at least a week's notice of a booking, or two weeks if they want to sell drink.

## What it costs
- Main Hall: £18 an hour for locals, £26 for people from outside, £38 for businesses. The smaller rooms cost less.
- Two hours is the shortest booking. Saturday night and whole-day deals often work out cheaper.
- Using the cooker and crockery is £15 on top. Making tea is free.
- Deposit: £150 for the Main Hall, £50 for a small room, £300 for big or boozy events.

## Kitchen and kit
- Tables and chairs are included. The stage, sound system and projector are extra.
- No fryers, no candles, no confetti, no smoke machines. Bouncy castles in the Main Hall only, with insurance.
- Leave it as you found it or we charge for cleaning.

## Drink, music and numbers
- The Main Hall holds 120 standing or 90 sitting down.
- Selling alcohol needs the committee's say-so two weeks ahead.
- Music off at 11pm, earlier on Sundays. Everyone out by half past eleven.

## Cancelling
- More than eight weeks ahead, they lose only £20. Inside four weeks they lose half; inside two weeks they lose the lot.
- The deposit always comes back on a cancellation.

## After the event
- Keys back by ten the next morning. Deposits usually go back within a fortnight.
