# Cancellations and Damage

Tannerwick Village Hall is a fictional organisation.

Adopted by the Management Committee on 3 March 2025. In force from 1 April 2025.

## 1 Cancellation by the hirer
1.1 Notice must be in writing, by letter or email, to the booking secretary. The date the notice is received is the date that counts.
1.2 Notice received more than 56 days before the hire date: everything paid is refunded, less a £20 administration charge.
1.3 Notice received 56 to 29 days before the hire date: the booking fee is kept and the rest is refunded.
1.4 Notice received 28 to 8 days before the hire date: 50% of the hire charge is kept.
1.5 Notice received 7 days or fewer before the hire date, or no notice at all: the whole hire charge is kept.
1.6 The damage deposit is refunded in full in every case. Extras that were not used are not charged.

## 2 Moving a hire
2.1 A hirer may move a hire to a new date once without charge, if the request is received at least 28 days before the original hire date and the new date is within 6 months of it.
2.2 Any other change of date is treated as a cancellation under section 1 followed by a new booking.

## 3 Cancellation by the hall
3.1 The hall may cancel a hire if the building is needed as a polling station or has to close for safety or repairs. It gives at least 14 days' notice where it can.
3.2 Everything paid is refunded in full. The hall pays no other compensation.

## 4 Deductions from the damage deposit
4.1 The hall is inspected within 2 days after the hire date.
4.2 The hall may deduct: repair or replacement at cost; cleaning at £30 an hour, with a minimum of 1 hour; overrun charges (hall-hire-charges.md, section 4.1); lost keys (hall-booking-and-deposits.md, section 4.2); and breakages (hall-kitchen-and-equipment.md, section 4).
4.3 For a breach of hall-licensing-limits.md the hall deducts £50 where music went on past the time allowed, and keeps the whole deposit where a capacity limit was exceeded.
4.4 An itemised list of deductions is sent to the hirer within 7 days after the hire date.
4.5 The rest of the deposit is returned within 14 days after the hire date. Costs above the deposit are invoiced and are payable within 30 days.

## 5 Disputes
5.1 A hirer who disagrees with a deduction writes to the chair within 14 days of the date on the itemised list.
5.2 The committee decides at its next meeting. Its decision is final.
