# Front Desk Quick Guide

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Last updated 1 December 2025. This sheet is a plain-words reminder, not the rules. If it disagrees with a governing document or with pool-changes.md, the governing documents and the changes log prevail. Check them before you promise anyone money back.

## Joining
- Adults and teenagers of 16 and up can join. Younger children from 8 can join as juniors with a parent who is a member. Under-8s come along as guests.
- An adult membership is about £26 a month, or £312 for the year. There is a joining fee of £40 on top.
- New members need a short induction before the first swim.
- If we are full, take their details for the waiting list.

## Renewing
- The year runs July to June. Renewals are due on 1 July and people have about three weeks to pay.
- If someone has let it lapse, they can usually come back within three months without paying the joining fee again.

## Booking
- Lane sessions are 45 minutes. Members can book about a week and a half ahead, up to 5 a week and one a day.
- Cancel at least 4 hours before and there is no charge.
- Miss a session without cancelling and it is a £4 charge. Three in a month and they lose booking for a fortnight.
- Lessons come in blocks of 10. Members get first pick for the first week of booking.

## Guests
- Two guests per member per visit. £6 for adults, £3 for children. The same guest can only come a handful of times a year, so check the guest book.

## Children
- Under-8s need an adult in the water with them, and one adult can take two.
- Older children can swim on their own once they have passed the 50-metre test. For under-12s a grown-up still has to stay in the building.

## Closures
- We shut for maintenance from 5 January 2026 to 16 January 2026, and on 25 December 2025, 26 December 2025 and 1 January 2026. No money back for those.
- If we have to shut without warning, bookings are cancelled and nobody is charged as a no-show.
- If there is no lifeguard after 15 minutes, the session is off.

## Refunds
- New members can change their mind in the first two weeks.
- Anyone leaving mid-year gets the unused whole months back, less a £20 charge, after a month's notice.
- Do not agree a refund at the desk. Pass it to the treasurer.
