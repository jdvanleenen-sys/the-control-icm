# Lifeguard Cover and Safety

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025.

## 1. Minimum cover
- **1.1** The pool does not open to swimmers unless at least 1 qualified lifeguard is on the poolside.
- **1.2** There must be 2 lifeguards on the poolside whenever more than 20 swimmers are in the water, or whenever a lesson for under-8s shares the pool with other swimmers.
- **1.3** No more than 40 swimmers may be in the water at once, whatever the number of lifeguards.

## 2. Qualifications
- **2.1** Every lifeguard holds a pool lifeguard qualification renewed within the last 24 months.
- **2.2** Every lifeguard completes at least 2 hours of in-pool training each calendar month. A lifeguard who misses 2 months in a row does not work on the poolside until they have caught up.
- **2.3** Lifeguards are aged 16 or over. A lifeguard under 18 is never the only lifeguard on duty.

## 3. Rotation
- **3.1** No lifeguard watches the water for more than 60 minutes without a break of at least 15 minutes away from the poolside.

## 4. When cover falls short
- **4.1** If a lifeguard is absent and no replacement arrives within 15 minutes of the session start, the session does not run.
- **4.2** If cover drops below section 1 during a session, the lifeguard clears the water at once.
- **4.3** A session lost this way is an unplanned closure and is handled under pool-closures-and-refunds.md section 2.

## 5. Early sessions
- **5.1** Sessions starting between 06:30 and 08:00 on weekdays are limited to 20 swimmers, all aged 16 or over, so that 1 lifeguard is enough under section 1.2.

## 6. Incidents and water checks
- **6.1** Every incident, however small, is written in the incident book on the day it happens.
- **6.2** The committee's safety officer reviews each entry within 7 days.
- **6.3** The water is tested every 2 hours while the pool is open. If free chlorine is outside 1.0 to 3.0 milligrams per litre the water is cleared until a repeat test passes.
- **6.4** A first-aid kit and a defibrillator are kept at the poolside door and checked on the first day of each month.
