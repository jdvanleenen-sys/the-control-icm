# Schedule of Fees (superseded, January to June 2025)

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

This schedule was in force from 1 January 2025 to 30 June 2025. It was replaced in full by pool-fees.md on 1 July 2025 and is kept only so that old payments can be checked. Do not use it for anything dated 1 July 2025 or later.

## 1. Membership fees
- **1.1** Annual fees: Adult £288, Junior £84, Household £612, Concession £210, Off-peak £186.
- **1.2** A member may pay in 12 monthly instalments. Each instalment is one-twelfth of the annual fee plus a £1.50 handling charge, so an Adult pays £25.50 a month.
- **1.3** Someone who joins part-way through the year pays one-twelfth of the annual fee for each whole calendar month left, not counting the month they join.

## 2. Joining fee
- **2.1** A one-off joining fee is paid with the first fee: £30 for Adult, Concession and Off-peak, and £45 for a Household. Juniors pay no joining fee.
- **2.2** Concession is for members aged 60 or over and for full-time students.

## 3. Pay-as-you-go charges
- **3.1** Guest fee: £5 for a guest aged 16 or over and £2.50 for a guest under 16, for each visit.
- **3.2** Lane sessions are free to members.
- **3.3** Group lessons: £70 for a block of 10 weekly lessons for members and £90 for non-members.
- **3.4** Private lessons: £22 for 30 minutes, members only.

## 4. Other charges
- **4.1** No-show charge: £3 for each missed lane session.
- **4.2** Failed instalment: £5 each time a monthly payment is returned unpaid.
- **4.3** Replacement membership card: £3.
- **4.4** Any charge left unpaid for 60 days stops the member booking until it is paid.

## 5. How to pay
- **5.1** Fees are paid by card at the front desk or by bank transfer. Cash is accepted for any amount.

## 6. Review
- **6.1** The committee reviews this schedule every May. The review held on 20 May 2025 led to the schedule now in pool-fees.md.
