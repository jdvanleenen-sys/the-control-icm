# Schedule of Fees

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025. It replaced the schedule kept in pool-fees-2025-archive.md.

## 1. Membership fees
- **1.1** Annual fees for the membership year 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026 are: Adult £312, Junior £96, Household £660, Concession £228, Off-peak £204. The categories are defined in pool-membership.md section 2.
- **1.2** A member may pay in 12 monthly instalments. Each instalment is one-twelfth of the annual fee plus a £2 handling charge, so an Adult pays £28 a month and a Household pays £57 a month.
- **1.3** Someone who joins part-way through the year pays one-twelfth of the annual fee for each whole calendar month left, counting the month they join. An Adult joining in October 2025 pays for 9 months, which is £234.

## 2. Joining fee
- **2.1** A one-off joining fee is paid with the first fee: £40 for Adult, Concession and Off-peak, and £60 for a Household. Juniors pay no joining fee.
- **2.2** The joining fee is waived for a lapsed member who rejoins in time under pool-membership.md section 4.3.

## 3. Pay-as-you-go charges
- **3.1** Guest fee: £6 for a guest aged 16 or over and £3 for a guest under 16, for each visit.
- **3.2** Lane sessions are free to members. There is no charge for booking.
- **3.3** Group lessons: £75 for a block of 10 weekly lessons for members and £95 for non-members.
- **3.4** Private lessons: £24 for 30 minutes, members only.

## 4. Other charges
- **4.1** No-show charge: £4 for each missed lane session, as described in pool-booking.md section 4.
- **4.2** Failed instalment: £8 each time a monthly payment is returned unpaid.
- **4.3** Replacement membership card: £5.
- **4.4** Any charge left unpaid for 30 days stops the member booking until it is paid.

## 5. How to pay
- **5.1** Fees are paid by card at the front desk, by card online or by bank transfer.
- **5.2** The front desk does not take more than £20 in cash for any one payment.

## 6. Review
- **6.1** The committee reviews this schedule every May.
- **6.2** New annual fees are announced by 1 June and take effect on 1 July. Other charges may be changed during the year with 30 days' notice, and any such change is recorded in pool-changes.md.
