# Membership Rules

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025.

## 1. Who can join
- **1.1** Anyone aged 16 or over may apply to be a full member.
- **1.2** A child aged 8 to 15 may be a junior member, but only if an adult member lives at the same address.
- **1.3** Children under 8 cannot be members. They swim as guests of an adult member under section 7 and pool-rules.md section 3.
- **1.4** The club has no more than 600 full members at any time. When the club is full, applicants go on a waiting list in order of the date their application was received.

## 2. Categories
- **2.1** The categories are Adult, Junior, Household, Concession and Off-peak. The fee for each is in pool-fees.md section 1.
- **2.2** A Household membership covers up to 2 adults and up to 3 juniors living at one address.
- **2.3** Concession is for members aged 65 or over and for full-time students who show a current student card.
- **2.4** Off-peak members may swim on weekdays between 09:00 and 15:00 only.

## 3. Joining
- **3.1** Apply on the club form with proof of address dated within the last 3 months.
- **3.2** Every new member attends a 30-minute induction before their first swim.
- **3.3** Membership starts on the day the first fee is paid, not the day of application.
- **3.4** The membership year runs from 1 July to 30 June.

## 4. Renewal and lapse
- **4.1** Renewal fees are due on 1 July each year.
- **4.2** There is a grace period of 21 days. A membership still unpaid at the end of 22 July lapses.
- **4.3** A lapsed member may rejoin within 90 days of the lapse without paying the joining fee again. After 90 days they apply as a new member and section 1.4 applies.

## 5. Freezing
- **5.1** A member may freeze their membership once in a membership year, for 1, 2 or 3 whole calendar months, by giving 14 days' written notice.
- **5.2** Frozen months are credited as set out in pool-closures-and-refunds.md section 4.3. A frozen member cannot book or swim.

## 6. Leaving and suspension
- **6.1** A member may resign by giving 30 days' written notice to the membership secretary.
- **6.2** The committee may suspend a member under pool-rules.md section 6.

## 7. Guests
- **7.1** A full member may bring up to 2 guests on any one visit and must stay with them throughout. The same person may come as a guest no more than 6 times in a membership year. Guest fees are in pool-fees.md section 3.
