# Pool Rules

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025.

## 1. Before you swim
- **1.1** Shower before entering the water.
- **1.2** Do not swim within 48 hours of sickness or diarrhoea, or with an open wound.
- **1.3** No outdoor shoes, food or glass on the poolside.

## 2. In the water
- **2.1** Lanes are marked slow, medium and fast. Swim in the lane that matches your speed and move if a lifeguard asks.
- **2.2** The shallow end is 0.9 metres deep and the deep end is 2.0 metres. No diving where the water is less than 1.5 metres deep.
- **2.3** No running, pushing, ducking or breath-holding games.

## 3. Children
- **3.1** A child under 8 must have an adult aged 18 or over with them in the water. One adult may look after no more than 2 children under 8.
- **3.2** A child aged 8 to 15 may swim without an adult in the water only after passing the club swim test of 50 metres unaided.
- **3.3** For a child under 12 who has passed the test, a responsible adult must still stay in the building.
- **3.4** Children under 8 come as guests and count towards the guest limits in pool-membership.md section 7.

## 4. Changing rooms and lockers
- **4.1** Lockers are for use during your visit only and are emptied when the pool closes each day.
- **4.2** Items found are kept at the front desk for 28 days and then given away.

## 5. Photography
- **5.1** No photographs or video anywhere in the building without the duty manager's permission given that day.

## 6. Breaches
- **6.1** A lifeguard's instruction must be followed at once. The lifeguard may ask anyone to leave the water for the rest of a session.
- **6.2** A first breach brings a spoken warning. A second breach within 12 months brings a written warning. A third breach within the same 12 months brings a suspension of 28 days.
- **6.3** For violence, abuse of staff or volunteers, or putting another swimmer in danger, the duty manager may suspend a member on the spot for up to 90 days while the committee decides what to do.
- **6.4** A member may appeal in writing to the committee within 14 days of a written warning or suspension. The committee decides within 28 days of receiving the appeal, and its decision is final.
- **6.5** Fees are not refunded for a period of suspension; see pool-closures-and-refunds.md section 5.
