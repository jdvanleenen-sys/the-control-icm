# Closures and Refunds

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025.

## 1. Planned closures
- **1.1** The pool closes for maintenance for up to 14 days in each membership year, with at least 28 days' notice. In this year the closure runs from 5 January 2026 to 16 January 2026.
- **1.2** The pool is also closed on 25 December 2025, 26 December 2025 and 1 January 2026.
- **1.3** No refund or credit is given for the closures in 1.1 and 1.2. If maintenance runs past 14 days, each extra day is credited at 1/365 of the member's annual fee.

## 2. Unplanned closures
- **2.1** An unplanned closure is any closure without 28 days' notice, such as plant failure, a failed water test or a shortage of lifeguards under pool-lifeguard-cover.md section 4.
- **2.2** Bookings for a closed session are cancelled by the club and do not count as no-shows or towards weekly booking limits.
- **2.3** If the pool is shut for more than 7 days in a row, each day from day 8 is credited at 1/365 of the member's annual fee. Credits come off the next renewal.

## 3. Lessons
- **3.1** A lesson cancelled by the club is added to the end of the block. If that cannot be done within 21 days of the block's planned last lesson, one-tenth of the block fee is refunded.
- **3.2** A block cancelled before it starts is refunded in full within 14 days.
- **3.3** A learner who withdraws before the third lesson is refunded for the lessons left, less £10. After the third lesson there is no refund unless a medical letter is supplied.

## 4. Membership refunds and credits
- **4.1** A new member may cancel within 14 days of paying and get everything back, including the joining fee. If they have swum, £6 is kept for each visit.
- **4.2** A member who resigns under pool-membership.md section 6.1 is refunded for each whole calendar month left after the notice ends, less £20. The joining fee is not refunded.
- **4.3** Each month frozen under pool-membership.md section 5 is credited at one-twelfth of the annual fee against the next renewal. It is not paid out in cash.

## 5. No refund
- **5.1** Nothing is refunded for a period of suspension under pool-rules.md section 6, for a no-show charge, or for a guest fee once the guest has swum.

## 6. Claims and payment
- **6.1** A claim must reach the treasurer within 60 days of the event. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 21 days of approval.

## 7. Matters not covered
- **7.1** These papers do not cover: hire of the pool by outside groups or for parties; galas and competitions; the car park and cycle racks; the vending machines; or how the club holds members' personal details. Ask the committee secretary about any of these.
