# Booking Rules

Quillmere Community Pool Club is a fictional organisation; these papers are made up.

Adopted by the general meeting on 14 June 2025. In force from 1 July 2025.

## 1. Lane sessions
- **1.1** Lane sessions last 45 minutes. The pool is open from 06:30 to 21:00 on weekdays and from 08:00 to 18:00 on Saturdays and Sundays.
- **1.2** A member may book a lane session up to 7 days ahead. Booking for a given day opens at 07:00.
- **1.3** A member may hold no more than 4 lane bookings in a week, counted Monday to Sunday, and no more than 1 on any day.
- **1.4** There are 4 lanes and no more than 6 swimmers in a lane, so a lane session takes 24 swimmers at most.
- **1.5** Off-peak members may only book sessions inside the hours given in pool-membership.md section 2.4.

## 2. Lessons
- **2.1** Group lessons run in blocks of 10 weekly lessons. Booking opens 21 days before a block starts.
- **2.2** For the first 7 days of booking only current members, including junior members, may book. After that, places are open to non-members.
- **2.3** A group has at most 8 learners, or 6 where the learners are under 8.
- **2.4** A block needs at least 4 learners. If fewer have booked 3 days before the start, the block is cancelled under pool-closures-and-refunds.md section 3.2.

## 3. Cancelling a booking
- **3.1** A lane booking may be cancelled without charge up to 4 hours before the session starts.
- **3.2** A cancelled booking does not count towards the weekly limit in section 1.3.
- **3.3** A single missed lesson is not refunded or moved, except as pool-closures-and-refunds.md section 3 allows.

## 4. No-shows
- **4.1** A member who has not checked in within 10 minutes of the start of a lane session is a no-show and the place is released.
- **4.2** Each no-show carries the charge set in pool-fees.md section 4.1.
- **4.3** Three no-shows within any 30 days suspend the member's right to book for 14 days. The member may still swim in a session that has free places on the day.
- **4.4** A session cancelled by the club is never a no-show.

## 5. Session waiting list
- **5.1** When a session is full a member may join its waiting list.
- **5.2** A member offered a place has 2 hours to accept, or until 1 hour before the session if that is sooner.

## 6. Guests
- **6.1** A guest's place must be booked by the member in the same booking. The limits in pool-membership.md section 7 apply.
